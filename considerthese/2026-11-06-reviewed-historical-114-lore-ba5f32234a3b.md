# Observation and imaginative experience

Walter Besant’s [The Art of Fiction](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/76460/pg76460-images.html), April 25, 1884 lecture, experience discussion and notes, ties invention to observation while qualifying the rule for historical fiction.

Henry James’s [response](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/58471/pg58471-images.html), 1884, experience discussion, contests rigid limits on experience: imagination can develop scant impressions without abandoning particularity. This disagreement does not license fabricated factual authority. Historical class restrictions are not endorsed.
