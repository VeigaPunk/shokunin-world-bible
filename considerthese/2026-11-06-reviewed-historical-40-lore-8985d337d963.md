# Disclose the geography an action needs

[Lessing's Laocoon](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/73078/pg73078-images.html), Frothingham translation, has an 1890 title page and 1873 copyright. Chapters XVI–XVII contrast simultaneous visual arrangement with successive language, allowing bodies through actions. Retaining sequential details is an aesthetic argument, not measured cognitive capacity or a ban on description.

[Ryan's Space](https://www-archiv.fdm.uni-hamburg.de/lhn/node/55/revisions/345/view.html), revised 2014 from 2012, distinguishes immediate frames, setting, mentioned locations and imaginatively completed worlds. Panoramic surveys and mobile routes disclose space differently. Mental geography need not match a graphic map's precision everywhere; relationships strategically required by events deserve particular attention.

This editorial comparison is not evidence of a direct rebuttal. Mark the narrow access or visibility relation a scene requires, then distinguish unsupported geography from contradiction and harmless omission. No reader study or tested adaptation performed.
