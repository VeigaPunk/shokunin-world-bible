# Author the intervention before optimizing it

[Weyhrauch1997](https://cs.uky.edu/~sgware/reading/papers/weyhrauch1997guiding.pdf) maximizes at managerial choices and averages at user choices. Abstract moves need authored concrete refinements. Manipulation penalties express artistic estimates, not correctness proofs. Search evaluations use simulated users; live-world integration remains incomplete.

[Trajectory targeting2006](https://expressiveintelligence.github.io/papers/Roberts_etal_-_TTD-MDP_-_AAAI06.pdf) aims at distributions over complete paths rather than maximum reward. Some targets are unattainable; sampled trees need a recovery policy for unseen trajectories.

[Nelson and Mateas2008](https://expressiveintelligence.github.io/papers/Nelson_Mateas_-_SBDM_-_AAAI08.pdf) distinguish objectives from optimization. Cached search and trajectory targeting share tree processing but back up different quantities. Varying which outcome is forced does not give players control. Options and choices remain rough agency measures, not validated universal scores; simulated comparisons do not establish player satisfaction.

Record permitted changes and preserved refusals separately from pacing goals. Unopened objects may already be constrained by dialogue.
