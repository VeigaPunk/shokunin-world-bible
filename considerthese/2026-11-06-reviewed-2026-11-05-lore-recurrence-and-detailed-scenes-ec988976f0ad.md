# When routine becomes a scene

Proust’s [Swann’s Way](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/7178/7178-h/7178-h.htm), Combray’s Sunday visits, moves between habitual framing and detailed conversation. Read the Léonie–Françoise exchange through Eulalie’s departure. Scott Moncrieff translation; Gutenberg transcription displays Henry Holt, 1922, facsimile unchecked. HTML/catalogue release dates disagree; both record update 2023.

Genette’s [Narrative Discourse](https://15orient.com/files/genette-on-narrative-discourse.pdf), pp.113–123, separates repetition of events from repetition of telling. His “pseudo-iterative” names richly particular scenes presented as recurring. He treats classical examples as figurative but argues Proust resists that reduction. This is an interpretation, not proof of authorial intention.

Ask what recurrence the passage asks us to accept, rather than how many paragraphs a routine deserves. A continuity summary should preserve that interpretive uncertainty instead of silently multiplying one exchange into identical dated transcripts.
