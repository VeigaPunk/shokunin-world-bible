# Lore: renewed perception is not automatic improvement

[Shklovsky](https://maenglishselfstudy.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/paper-v-section-2-art-as-a-technique-viktor-shklovsky-text.pdf),1917, contrasts habitual recognition with renewed perception. Difficulty and delayed recognition can be artistic means; unnamed familiar objects are one technique, not a demand to obscure every sentence. The scan is attributed to the1965 Lemon/Reis translation but lacks printing verification and a permissive license.

[Miall and Kuiken](https://sites.ualberta.ca/~dmiall/LiteraryReading/Readings/Miall%20Kuiken%20Foregrounding.pdf),1994, connect foregrounding with reading time, strikingness and affect across four studies of three stories. Correlations do not establish the proposed causal sequence. Reading time cannot identify the interpretive activity; stylistic components behave differently across stories. Unusual language is not thereby proven better writing.

Keep factual continuity separate from descriptive emphasis, and retain plain descriptions of consequential mechanisms beside expressive passages. This editorial recommendation is not a reader-study result. No exercise executed.
