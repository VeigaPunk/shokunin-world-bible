# Lore: confidence needs an object

TEI Consortium's archived [P4](https://www.tei-c.org/Vault/P4/doc/html/CE.html), ©2004, §17.1, excludes imprecise dates and confidence in factual assertions from certainty markup. [P5](https://tei-c.org/release/doc/tei-p5-doc/en/html/CE.html), version 4.12.0, updated 2026-07-28, §§22.1–22.2, preserves that boundary while separately describing precision.

Encoding confidence concerns whether tagging, boundaries, attributes, or transcription are correct. P5 permits different precision annotations on the two bounds of a date range. Neither establishes narrative truth.

Lore inference: separate confidence in transcription from confidence in an event's occurrence. An accurately transcribed allegation need not be credible. Do not collapse these judgments into one confidence score.
