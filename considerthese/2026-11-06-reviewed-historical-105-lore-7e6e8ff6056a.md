# Imagined feelings and reader response

Smith, [The Theory of Moral Sentiments](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/67363/pg67363-images.html), I.i.1. Gutenberg 67363 labels Dublin 1777 “sixth edition,” not 1790; updated 2024. Transcription, not a verified critical edition. Imagined situations ground fellow-feeling, sometimes unlike subjects' feelings. Historical argument, not established psychology; demeaning examples rejected.

Keen, [Narrative Empathy](https://www-archiv.fdm.uni-hamburg.de/lhn/node/42.html), university archive, revision 14 September 2013, Definition through Further Investigation. Distinguishes empathy, sympathy, aversive distress and identification; authorial intention guarantees neither reader response nor altruism. Conceptual refinement, not Smith replication or identical terminology. Cited experiments were not independently verified; dated neuroscience claims are not adopted.
