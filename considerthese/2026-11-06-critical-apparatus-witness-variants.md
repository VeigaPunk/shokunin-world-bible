# Lore: preserve variants before choosing a reading

[TEI Consortium's critical apparatus](https://tei-c.org/release/doc/tei-p5-doc/en/html/TC.html), P5 version4.12.0, updated2026-07-28, describes a refinement from compressed printed apparatus to structured variation supporting interactive witness selection. No superior editing is established.

For a world bible, conflicting editions need not be flattened into one corrected sentence. Keep the witness that contains a reading separate from the scholar asserting it and the editor preferring it. A reconstructed reading and an attested reading have different evidence.

The chapter leaves grouping and editorial methodology to editors. Selecting a base text remains optional; encoding alternatives does not establish which version is canon. Comparison still requires choosing relevant variations and witnesses.

Selected introductory and reading sections read, not the whole Guidelines. Existing TEI references cover dates and names; this proposed use concerns edition variants. No implementation, training or assimilation.
