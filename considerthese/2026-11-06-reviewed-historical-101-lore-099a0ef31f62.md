# Rigidity and benign violations

Bergson, [Laughter](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/4352/pg4352-images.html), Chapter I introduction/§§I–II, Brereton/Rothwell translation: emotional detachment and rigidity organize a social account, offered as a leitmotiv rather than complete definition. Reject its demeaning generalizations. Gutenberg 4352: released 2003, updated 2020; print edition unidentified. Historical catalogue check records US public domain; jurisdiction and ebook conditions apply.

McGraw/Warren, [Benign Violations](https://leeds-faculty.colorado.edu/mcgrawp/pdf/mcgraw.warren.2010.pdf), Psychological Science 2010, introduction/Discussion/Conclusion: simultaneous benignness and violation offer a different account; reported moral-scenario studies allow amusement alongside disgust. This is conceptual contrast, not Bergson replication, universal prediction or moral permission.
