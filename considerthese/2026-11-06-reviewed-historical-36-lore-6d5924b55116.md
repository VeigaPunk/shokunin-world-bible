# Viewpoint shifts need local expectations

[Genette](https://15orient.com/files/genette-on-narrative-discourse.pdf),1980 English translation of1972 French material, distinguishes narrator from organizing perspective. The paperback scan is not authenticated as first printing. Focalization may describe a short passage, not an entire book. Deliberate changes differ from local departures against a surrounding pattern. First-person narration need not restrict knowledge to the protagonist's past experience.

[Niederhoff's2012 reply](https://www-archiv.fdm.uni-hamburg.de/lhn/comment/51.html#comment-51) distinguishes information restriction from evaluative presentation, allowing overlap. Narrator commentary can qualify a character's experience by degrees. He recognizes earlier gradualist precedents: this is theoretical refinement, not consensus or a mechanical classifier.

The distinction prevents confusing who narrates with whose experience organizes a passage. Review information and evaluation separately before alleging a continuity defect; changed stance need not add an event. Interpretive categories cannot authorize inventing canon.
