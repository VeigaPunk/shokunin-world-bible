theme-index of the 22 notes, an explicit "considerthese ≠ admitted" line,

queue, anchor spot-checks, reading-queue status table, confidence tags,
dry-merge preview) still stand and aren't repeated. New ones from reading
the notes and from today's quiet:

- **Score new notes against the pairing-and-refusal template.** Check
  whether a note names both sides and refuses supersession, reuse, and
  evidence-collapse, so outliers show up instead of another bag of
  filenames. This is a method check, not a theme index.
- **Tag transcription certainty, not just license.** Many notes already
  flag Gutenberg HTML vs catalogue date disagreements, unverified
  facsimiles, and "not a verified critical edition" (Proust, Besant/James,
  Aristotle in `…-historical-45-…`, Radcliffe in `…-historical-118-…`). A
  small certainty mark (transcription / unverified facsimile / pinned
  edition) would calibrate how much weight I give a cited passage without
  re-fetching unless you ask.
- **Treat "Optional exercises omitted" as a stall, not a footer.** Every
  historical note in the batch ends that way. After two quiet days, the
  unused exercises are the next depth gain — for example the untested
  Austen speech change-list in `…-historical-84-…`, or recording permitted
  changes separately from pacing goals in `…-historical-27-…`. I would
  list them, not run them.
- **Report synced-as-of, not just ahead/behind.** After each of my
  reflection commits, a previously identical curation branch will look
  "behind." Saying "synced as of `c6bbd86`, now 1 reflection behind"
  keeps that from reading like abandoned competing work.
- **Keep rejected examples separate from kept distinctions.** The humor
  and fellow-feeling notes (`…-historical-101-…`, `…-historical-105-…`)
  reject demeaning generalizations while retaining rigidity vs
  benign-violation, and imagined fellow-feeling vs later empathy/sympathy
  splits. Summaries should not treat those sources as wholesale
  endorsements or wholesale discards.
