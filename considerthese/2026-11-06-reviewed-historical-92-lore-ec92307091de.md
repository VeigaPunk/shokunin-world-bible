# Lore: crossing representational boundaries

[Pirandello, Six Characters](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/42148/pg42148-images.html), Storer translation, depicts characters interrupting rehearsing actors to seek an author and offer their drama. This is fictional practice, not evidence of audience response. The ebook displays a 1922 imprint alongside a later Nobel notice; exact printing remains unverified.

[Pier, Metalepsis, 2016 revision](https://www-archiv.fdm.uni-hamburg.de/lhn/node/51.html) analyzes transgressions between representational levels. Mere nesting is insufficient. Its transmedial discussion expands the analytical vocabulary while retaining differences among definitions and effects; it supplies no universal recipe for surprise.

The earlier work remains useful as a concrete artistic case; later analysis helps distinguish boundary crossing from ordinary embedding. Selected passages only, not whole works. Gutenberg's US public-domain status does not settle other jurisdictions or remove ebook terms. Hamburg copyright; permissive reuse unverified. Commentary and links only.
