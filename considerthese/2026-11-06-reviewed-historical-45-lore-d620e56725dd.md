# Lore: change and consequence are different questions

[Aristotle's Poetics, Butcher translation](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/1974/1974-h/1974-h.htm), distinguishes connected action from everything happening to one protagonist. Reversal and recognition should arise from preceding action. This is tragic-composition prescription, not a universal narrative requirement.

The HTML gives electronic dates2008/2013; the catalogue gives1999/2016. Neither authenticates a print edition or dates the ancient argument. The catalogue declares United States public-domain status; jurisdiction and Gutenberg license conditions still matter.

[Hühn's Event and Eventfulness](https://www-archiv.fdm.uni-hamburg.de/lhn/node/39/revisions/152/view.html), revised August2013, distinguishes any state change from an interpretively noteworthy event. Expectations and context matter; significance may differ for participants, narrator and reader. This authored synthesis is not a universal quality score or independent verification of every cited theory.

Ask what changed and why the chapter treats it as consequential. A continuity record cannot decide significance; a quiet chapter need not become a crisis. No reader study or adaptation performed.
