## Findings & named debt

- **C2 — improved, but the raw-JSON coverage receipt remains incomplete.** Both candidates fail a renamed-header/drop attack; both prevent a retelling miss from increasing source support; M2 also warns on unreferenced excerpt declarations, updates the r89 paraphrase map, and reports distinct anchors and row counts. Prefer M2 as the integration base over M1: M1 leaves an unreferenced Spence mode flip silent and makes the unchanged r89 map fail for missing Inventory. M2 fails duplicate rows (rather than merely warning), which satisfies the requested WARN/FAIL surfacing; its failing receipt must not be read as a pass.
- **D-C7-1 — `JSON-FACTS 14/14` is a row receipt, not a raw JSON census.** Raw r87 contains 22 source-attributed obligations (7 claims, 7 first-appearance pointers, 4 relations, 4 events). Its map has 14 JSON-category rows plus 8 editorial rows. Six first-appearance pointers are co-mapped to entity rows, while two other JSON facts are only described in prose. The total of 22 mapped rows therefore happens to equal the 22 raw obligations but counts different things; only 20 raw obligations are represented by table rows, and two are not anchor-validated by the command. R88’s 10 rows do match its 10 raw obligations. Keep the row/fact distinction explicit and do not present r87’s `14/14` or `22/22` row total as full 22-fact support.
- **D-C7-2 — expected inventory is self-declared.** The M2 checker compares the map’s declared counts to parsed map rows, not to raw JSON records. That closes accidental header/drop/padding failures while the declaration remains intact; it does not independently prove that the declaration or map covers every source-attributed JSON obligation. The M3 wording and M2’s map identify the two prose-only crosswalks, but they do not turn them into validated rows.
- **C2 WARN semantics do not inflate source support in the retelling probe.** M2 reports `SOURCE-SUPPORT 0/0`, retelling `0/1`, and `WARN: ... excluded from source support`; its `RESULT PASS WITH WARNINGS` is a lexical-preflight completion status, not source support. The M1 variant also leaves its source numerator at zero, but lacks M2’s orphan-mode guard.
- **C3 — edge proposals comply with PROVINCE and meet the required shape.** R8 says a failed/disputed conjunct makes R2 unavailable but does not prove source use; it resolves detail-vs-element withhold precedence, keeps disputes open, and closes the provisional-note lifecycle. R9 requires an item-specific, reliable link to the actual delivered reprint and limits support to that printing. R10 scopes `source_id` to `(cluster-relative path, source_id)`, treats a repeated string as a namespace collision rather than identity, routes comparisons to the incoming README, and does not put ID collisions alone in the live register. Each edge has an owner, trigger, worked example, and verification command. No decision claims canon from resemblance, silently resolves an in-world contradiction, or verifies game mechanics.
- **No rights/training merge.** D1’s proposed table keeps Attribution, Rights status, and AI-training permission in distinct columns; its R8/R9/R10 prose says the evidence does not decide rights or imply permission. The worked R8 sequence is not found in corpus canon (`Grep "Kesh Hymn" corpus/` returned no matches); it is a policy example, not a new canon record.
