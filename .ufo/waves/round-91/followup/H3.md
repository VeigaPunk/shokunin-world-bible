## Findings & named debt

1. **The unchanged checker reproduces r90 byte-for-byte.** All 24 r90 rows — 16 replayed mutations,
   4 probes, 4 C9 controls — re-run against the delivered tree match the committed r90 receipts on
   every compared field: stdout bytes, exit codes, `fixture_sha256` (=> the rebuilt fixtures are
   themselves byte-identical), disclosure labels/causes, structural assertions, and the
   r89-vs-r90 flags. 0 silent passes, 0 assertion failures, 0 exit flips. r90's mutation-sanity
   finding therefore still stands at c2ab3d0 without re-argument.
2. **The two new fixtures are discriminating exactly as claimed, and only they differ.** Across all
   166 directories, the e97613c and delivered checkers differ on stdout only for
   `r91-c1-null-chain` and `r91-c1-tick-unresolved`, each with exactly one changed output line (the
   repaired null-chain line and the repaired tick line); exit codes are identical (1) for both
   directories, and every other line — including the unresolved-holder chain-break line — is
   byte-identical between checkers. A silent revert of either gate would change these two durable
   fixtures' outputs, which is the property C1 designed them for.
3. **README pins verified from the delivered tree.** Both fixtures' complete stdout under both
   checkers equals the README "Complete stdout" blocks byte-for-byte (4/4 comparisons), and the
   C9-watch probe commands reproduce the pinned definite/qualified wording. No drift between the
   documentation pins and the shipped behavior.
4. **Census consistent.** `ca7f9ef` 164 dirs → `c2ab3d0` 166 dirs, +2 / −0, no pre-existing
   fixture JSON touched; the ten new JSON files are the two fixtures' five sections each.
5. **Named debt H3-r91-D1 (scratch-only harness).** The adapted replay harness and the raw scan
   capture live under `/tmp/r91-H3` (as in r90, the harness is not a durable artifact). The
   receipts are archived in this lane: `followup/H3-r91-replay.json` (all 24 rows, full stdout) and
   `followup/H3-r91-scan.json` (166-row summary + the two differing dirs in full). If the scratch
   is swept, a future replay needs the same three substitutions on a re-derived r90 harness.
6. **Named debt H3-r91-D2 (coverage shape, unchanged from r89/r90).** This seat exercises the
   checker through fixtures only; the non-provenance branches (timeline double-booking,
   contradictions, allied_with reciprocals) remain outside the mutation set, as noted by r89/r90
   H3. No new gap introduced here.
