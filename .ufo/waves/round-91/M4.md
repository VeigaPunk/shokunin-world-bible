## Findings & named debt

- **C2 improved, but not fully aligned with every literal attack requirement in either proposal.** Both candidate variants fail closed on a renamed JSON ID header and a missing Inventory declaration, split JSON/editorial/total receipts, and warn on a retelling anchor miss. They preserve green normal r87/r88 receipts (M2's r88 run additionally warns on the existing unused Spence excerpt declaration).
- **D-M4-1 — M1 misses the unreferenced-mode attack.** With the Spence declaration mode flipped from `verbatim` to `summary`, M1 prints the unchanged `10/10` receipt and `RESULT PASS` with no warning. M2 reports `WARN: unreferenced excerpt declaration: source-excerpt.md (mode=summary)` and `RESULT PASS WITH WARNINGS`.
- **D-M4-2 — M2 hard-fails duplicates but does not emit the requested WARN.** The duplicate is surfaced as `DUPLICATE-ROWS 1` and `FAIL: duplicate mapped row P1 appears 2 times` (exit 1), along with row inventory mismatch. M1 emits the explicit duplicate-row warning and fails inventory (exit 1). If the requirement is literally a warning in addition to detection, add a `WARN` to M2's duplicate path or use M1's warning behavior alongside M2's orphan-mode handling.
- **Distinct-anchor interpretation:** duplicating an identical row cannot numerically reduce the unique-anchor set. Both candidates report 6 distinct anchors against 11 parsed rows after the duplicate; M2 additionally reports repeated references rising from 4 to 5 and total rows 11/10. The distinct count is not inflated by the duplicate, but it stays 6 rather than decreasing.
- **Receipt denominator remains rows, not JSON facts.** The split is explicit: M1 reports `JSON-FACTS 14/14`, `EDITORIAL 8/8`, `TOTAL 22/22`; M2 reports `RECEIPT JSON-FACTS 14; EDITORIAL-ROWS 8; TOTAL-ROWS 22`. Do not describe r87's total 22 as 22 JSON facts.
