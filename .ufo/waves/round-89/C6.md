## Findings & named debt

- **A — isolated and bounded on the old corpus.** It changes only the valid-key multi-cause summary in `r87-probe-multi-invalid`; all five violations remain, exit stays 1. Its list/join behavior preserves all causes that actually latch on the record; it does not expand the shape-rejected early-exit branch.
- **B — zero old-corpus delta, regression case verified, but qualification is too broad.** F3 proves the unresolved raw-duplicate case is qualified while the unique-ID control is unchanged. However, X1's `holder_identity_detail(None, ...)` treats the schema-valid sentinel `from_holder: null` as an unresolved entity (`0 raw / 0 indexed`). At a later record this causes both a definite `None`-versus-previous-holder chain break and an independent tick inversion to be labeled identity-uncertain. Scratch reproduction (cwd `/tmp/lore-c6-final-x1.s12vs89l`, command `python3 corpus/fixtures/check_bible.py corpus/fixtures/r87-chain-unique-broken-null-holder-probe`) returned exit 1 and emitted `from_holder None is not uniquely resolvable (0 raw entity rows ... 0 is in the resolved index)` on both findings. Separately, the new `transition_holder_details` gate qualifies a tick-order finding solely because a transition holder is unresolved; holder resolution is not a cause of `tick <= previous_tick`. First-record `from_holder != null` is also a definite condition, not an identity comparison. Restrict the B predicate to non-null IDs and to identity-dependent chain continuity; leave tick ordering and the first-record null requirement definite unless another independent cause applies.
- **C — isolated, raw distinction measured.** Three baseline tuples change because their first-stop source IDs are absent from both raw sources and the resolved index; the synthetic raw-present invalid-source test confirms the other wording. This does not alter local unknown/dangling diagnostics, the error count, or exits. Unavailable tables remain a separate cause.

In the 149 census all 31 clean and 118 dirty exits are preserved, violation-line count remains 367, and stderr remains empty. F1/F2/F3 runs all remain exit 1. No clean-to-dirty or dirty-to-clean flip was observed.

A durable empty/malformed-source-ID fixture and expectation update are still needed. No fixture artifact was edited here.
