## Critique and recommendation (not execution evidence)

The calibration premise is plausible but narrower than “negative tick means no booking/conflict.” The checker explicitly declares negative ticks invalid, so calling two different-place events a **definite** booking based on their invalid shared coordinate overstates what the checker has validated. Yet equality and different places are still present in the raw input; suppressing the finding does not establish that there is no conflict. A diagnostic framed as conditional/raw-input structural evidence could be defensible instead. Avoid implying that invalid means fictional impossibility, event absence, or conflict-free.

Recommend the smallest candidate guard: preserve each negative row and its invalid-tick diagnostic, but exclude it from occupancy compare/store only. Keep nonnegative occupancy unchanged. This is a calibration improvement only if the product claim is specifically about avoiding an unqualified definite booking on an invalid tick; it is not a correctness proof about canon. Keep the finding wording and docs explicit about this boundary.

Before admission, require paired controls: (1) negative/negative distinct-place pair gets both invalid-tick diagnostics and no definite booking; (2) identical nonnegative pair still books; (3) negative row before, between, and after two valid same-actor/same-tick distinct-place rows does not suppress the valid pair; (4) negative row followed by valid event at the same actor but valid tick does not collide; (5) a single negative row is retained with its other diagnostics; (6) replay all 45 prior outputs exactly. Use fixture-input-only descriptions. A conditional raw-input warning could be considered separately, not added implicitly in this axis move.

No hidden conflict in an actual world can be resolved from these JSON fields; missing/ambiguous actor/place identities remain separate rules. The finite probes do not prove general event-pair detection or source/canon support. I make no axis-admission verdict because no changed candidate, full replay, reviewer/verifier, or independent adjudication was in scope here.
