**(a) Evidence to seek: common occasion.** Request independently attributable records for each reported reading/presence, each explicitly linking that report to one and the same specifically identified ringing/occasion. Inspect the source and its attribution, the exact occasion identifier, and the record’s scope/completeness (including whether the relevant interval or register is complete and whether it covers the asserted reading/presence). A match on “Bell-7,” a date, a tick, or one register statement is not enough to establish a common occasion. If records independently and adequately link both reports to that same identified ringing, that supports the shared-occasion premise; if they link to distinct occasions, dates conflict, or one/both records are incomplete, ambiguous, absent, or not independently attributable, common occasion remains unestablished. Even positive linkage supports only this premise, not whether either account is truthful or canonical.

Seek records that locate the person Tarin reports seeing at Harbor during the identified ringing, and records for Sella’s claimed presence/absence at Tower/Harbor during that same ringing; verify what each record directly covers and its completeness. Positive independent identification/location evidence would support those person/place premises only. Mere name/place labels, ledger pointers, or copied summaries do not independently identify the person or establish physical presence. Missing, non-specific, conflicting, or incomplete evidence leaves person/place unresolved; it does not establish either report true or false.

The shared ringing, the identity of the person Tarin says he saw, and physical presence at either place therefore remain unverified. This is an editorial evidence request, not an authenticity, canon, rights, or mechanics finding; it does not resolve the fictional reports.
