# Round 45 citation-scout — evidence and limits

## Finding

**Conditional plausibility, not a source-verified calibration defect.** A faction can plausibly have two outposts, and two reports at two places during the same local tick need not describe one singular character moving instantaneously. But the premise does not establish that this timeline's `entity_id` models a distributed faction, that *all* faction subjects are distributed, or that these citations authenticate either report. Recommend **no-go on a factual/canon conclusion**; only consider a narrow, explicitly editorial checker convention if the fixture and independent reviewers establish that timeline rows for factions denote presence/reporting rather than a faction's singular location. Do not exempt all noncharacters: items, places, and other factions may still have singular-location semantics.

## Source attribution boundaries / falsifiable decision

A fictional report can literally say that a named faction kept outposts at Harbor and Tower in the same named local watch. A clause map can align those words to two event rows and editorial entity/place IDs. That supports only the narrow statement **“this invented report says X”** and an editor's mapping choice. It does not establish that the fictional source is authentic, that the faction actually occupied both places, that both reports share an objective occasion, or that all faction entities have distributed presence. The source citation is a resolvable in-world ID, not independent corroboration.

**Go** only if a controlled pair shows the baseline faction conflict, a character conflict remains reported, and a non-author reviewer can point to an explicit, consistently applied fixture convention that timeline events encode faction presence at separate outposts (rather than a faction's single location). Keep any behavior scoped to that convention; preserve unknown/non-faction semantics and report calibration as a data-model choice. **No-go** if the only support is kind=`faction`, a constructed source excerpt, matching local ticks, a CLEAN result, or assumed distributed membership. If proceeding, make the test falsifiable with a same-faction/same-tick/two-place pair, a matched character conflict, and a faction singular-location control; state exactly which diagnostic changes. None of these outcomes establishes real-world facts, authenticity, chronology, canon, rights, or permission.
