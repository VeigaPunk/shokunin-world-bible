# Round 76 independence scout — attribution is not corroboration

## Scope

Read the r76 declaration in `.shokunin/lore/LOG.md`, the r73 Jorin excerpt/source/claim and comparison, the r74 evidence note, and relevant r72/r75 precedent. This is report-only. I made no corpus, LOG, checker, or other file changes, and no commits, pushes, or merges.

## What the existing material says

R73’s newly authored fictional excerpt says: “I was sent to copy a gate notice. At the east steps, I read “KEEP THE EAST GATE OPEN” on a slate. I did not see who put it there.” The source row calls `jorin-slate-note` a newly authored fictional, unverified first-person report; Jorin’s claim says he *reports* being sent, reading those words, and not seeing who placed it. These pointers connect the claim to that account. They do not authenticate the account, its assignment, a sender, slate, wording, or provenance. R73’s comparison and r74’s evidence note expressly leave inscription identity, reading times, and source relationship unresolved. R74 lists a provenance-preserving, independently attributable record only as evidence one might seek; it does not say such a record exists.

## Judgment: NO-GO as independent corroboration

A separately named, newly invented fictional registry record can provide a **second attribution in the authored fixture**: the fixture could say that a fictional registry entry reports a copy request directed to Jorin. It cannot, merely by being assigned another source ID, another document title/type, or an institutional-sounding author, count as **genuinely independent corroboration** of Jorin’s account. Those are authored metadata and narrative assertions, not demonstrated provenance or independence. The r76 declaration itself correctly warns that a new invented in-world record would not authenticate Jorin’s account; this scout agrees.

The proposed statement would also need precision about what the record directly says. A record of a request might support only “the entry records a request,” not that an actual sender gave it, that Jorin received or complied with it, that the entry was contemporaneous, or that it concerned the same notice/slate he reports copying. If the invented entry merely repeats Jorin’s account, quotes it, or derives from his note, it is compatible with copying/dependence rather than independent support. A different source ID cannot distinguish these possibilities. Even separate fictional authorship and an explicit attribution would establish only that the fixture presents two purported records/accounts, not that their origins are independent.

To describe a source as independent corroboration would require evidence of its origin and custody/provenance, an evidentiary basis independent of Jorin’s report, and a demonstrated link between the record and the specific proposition/occasion being corroborated. The inspected material supplies none of that. Inventing such details inside a fixture would still be new fictional attribution, not external authentication. If the round proceeds with a registry record, label the relation accurately as a newly invented, fictional, unverified record that *purports to record* a request; keep provenance and dependence unknown, and do not use “independent,” “corroborates,” “verified,” or equivalent language as a finding.

## Precedent and limits

R72’s source/citation precedent distinguishes a fixture citation (an attribution link) from authenticity. R75 is a useful caution against converting structural duplication or a second row into a substantive rule: exact repeated relation rows were accepted by the checker, but that alone could not tell intended repetition from a copy or justify a violation. Similarly, distinct registry and Jorin rows do not establish distinct evidentiary origins. R72’s report-level perspective and r73’s different reported wording can be discussed as attributed text; neither makes an additional source ID independent evidence.

**Axis assessment:** the record may add a narrowly distinct *fictional attributed perspective* if its contents ask a useful editorial question not already answered by r73/r74; this scout does not establish material depth. It adds no authenticated corroboration. No basis here to assert source truth, canon, rights, or game mechanics. A source row, clause map, clean checker result, or editorially invented provenance story cannot settle those matters.
