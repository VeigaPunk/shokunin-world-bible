2. **Occasion laundering:** repeated “Bell-7” wording or tick 3 can look like synchronization. Keep occasion attribution explicit and local tick labeled editorial/arbitrary; no clock conversion or simultaneity claim.
3. **Location overreach:** east steps are a specific reported location; map to broad `harbor` only as a fixture entity alignment, not the registry desk or objective coordinates.
4. **Absence overclaim:** Vela and Jorin's non-sightings cannot establish Sella/envelope absence.
5. **Bell overclaim:** r41 wording improves Vela's reported identification but does not establish an objective strike or who struck it.
6. **Citation/provenance confusion:** checker-valid `source_id` only ties a row to an ID; it cannot authenticate a fictional report or entail its claim. The r41 clause map is stale, so quote the excerpt itself and author a precise fresh map if used.
