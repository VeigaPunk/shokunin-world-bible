## Axis verdicts

- **Depth — unchanged.** The explicit acquisition and ordered transfer fields give the checker a new structural assertion to examine, but do not deepen the fictional canon, establish actual custody, verify source contents, or build a general provenance ontology. No old depth behavior is shown harmed.
- **Coverage — improved.** The baseline has no item-chain test. The active checker now checks an optional item-local acquisition and successive cited transfer records for unambiguous holder IDs, strictly increasing local ticks, and immediate predecessor agreement. The paired raw fixtures and independent CLI replay isolate a valid chain from a broken chain; old cases retain exact baseline outputs. Absent provenance remains permitted; malformed present values are reported, not inferred or repaired. This is coverage of the declared structural convention, not of all item histories.
- **Calibration — unchanged.** The narrow rule and documentation explicitly distinguish chain inconsistency from factual falsity, treat equal/reversed ticks as ambiguous/order violations, and avoid forcing unrelated bibles to provide a provenance list. A resolvable source ID checks membership only: the fixture's `archive-ledger` description does **not** independently attest either transfer, and the first acquisition is likewise an assertion, not a proven historical first holder. Duplicate source IDs still produce a global duplicate error while last-write-wins lookup lets chain comparisons proceed; the rule therefore cannot claim unambiguous citation identity in that case. Those are real limits, not grounds to call cited assertions validated. The `CLEAN` summary omits provenance, so it is underinclusive rather than proof of transfer semantics. The original integrity overclaim is corrected, not silently accepted; the fresh scoped replay supports the final stability claim. No demonstrated new false-positive semantic rule or source-truth claim warrants a harmed verdict, but neither is calibration improvement proved.

## Admission

**Admit round 6: coverage improved; depth and calibration unchanged; no axis shown harmed.** This judgment is limited to the declared optional, explicitly listed chain convention and tested inputs. It does not assert completeness, text-level source support, unique source resolution under duplicate IDs, actual transfer/custody, canon truth, or mechanics verification. No code, fixture, or canon correction follows from the verdict.
