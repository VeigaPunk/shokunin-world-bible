## Risks / citation boundary

A future note can report that Sella says she handed the carbon to Jorin; the utterance would evidence only that newly authored report. It cannot authenticate that a physical transfer occurred, establish Jorin’s actual receipt, or independently verify that this is the same physical carbon beyond Sella’s identification. “I handed it to Jorin” is not equivalent to “Jorin received it.” The prior “kept” wording remains a report, not an independently corroborated custody record.

The blurred blue oval is explicitly unidentified and explicitly not attributed to Mira’s Blue Seal. It must not be renamed a seal, treated as Mira’s mark, used as transfer authentication, or promoted to an objective fact. Neither a green checker nor source-ID resolution validates prose entailment. The fictional citations establish neither canon nor real-world authorship/provenance. No rights, license, permission to reproduce, or AI-training permission follows from a fictional citation, a reference ledger entry, or authorship of fixture text.
