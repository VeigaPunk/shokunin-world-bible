# R29 citation scout: linkage is not authentication

## Evidence and distinction

The r28 excerpt is explicitly labeled “Newly authored fictional in-world excerpt” and gives only “I, Mira, carried the blue seal” (`.shokunin/lore/corpus/fixtures/r28-courier-excerpt/courier-log-excerpt.md:1-3`). Its map treats the `Mira`→`mira` and “blue seal”→`seal” correspondences as fixture-local editorial alignment, not textual entailment or external verification (`.shokunin/lore/corpus/fixtures/r28-courier-excerpt/relation-clause-map.md:7-13`). This establishes a fictional ID-linkage convention for interpreting the clause; it does not authenticate a historical record or establish that the document exists outside this authored fixture.

The JSON labels are fictional metadata, not authentication: `sources.json` calls the item a “fictional personal log” and describes its reliability as “in-world fiction; first-person account” (`.shokunin/lore/corpus/fixtures/r28-courier-excerpt/sources.json:1-13`). The reference ledger explicitly categorizes its citations as real-world method references, says method attribution is not story fact, and says story facts are separately cited within the bible (`.shokunin/lore/corpus/knowledge/reference-ledger.md:1-12`). Neither a source-ID match nor a plausible title/reliability note is independent evidence of source authenticity.

## Calibration risk

A checker or reviewer could collapse two separate questions: (1) does the supplied fictional wording map to the selected entity IDs under the stated editorial alignment? (2) is the cited source authentic or independently verified? The excerpt supports lexical `carries` conditional on that alignment (`relation-clause-map.md:11-13`), while the map disclaims external verification and canon (`relation-clause-map.md:7-9,15-17`). Conversely, lack of a supporting clause means a claim is **unsupported by this excerpt**, not false in canon; the map says other JSON claims are unsupported, not refuted (`relation-clause-map.md:15-17`). The timeline also cites `courier-log` (`timeline.json:1-22`), but this excerpt does not describe departure/arrival; the shared ID alone cannot transfer clause support to those claims.

## Falsifiable test

In a matched near-miss variant, keep all five r28 JSON tables and IDs byte-identical, replacing the excerpt sentence with “The blue seal was near Mira.” The map should retain editorial entity-ID links as stipulated, but label `relations[0].type=carries` **unsupported by this wording**: proximity does not entail carrying. It must not label the relation false or resolve canon. Both variants can pass structural checks because the checker does not read either Markdown file (`relation-clause-map.md:15-17`). A falsifying observation would be a non-author reviewer treating the near-miss as support for `carries`, treating lack of support as canon falsity, or claiming external authentication based solely on the fictional source ID/title/reliability metadata. The positive control is the literal “carried” clause, which supports the relation only conditional on editorial alignment, not source authenticity.
