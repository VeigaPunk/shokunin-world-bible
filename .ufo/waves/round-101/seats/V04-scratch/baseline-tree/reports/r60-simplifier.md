## Smallest distinction; recommendation

**Calibration GO, narrowly:** retain a per-table load status (at minimum loaded vs unavailable) rather than making failure indistinguishable from `[]`. For a reference whose target lookup depends on unavailable `entities`, suppress only the *absence-dependent* `dangling entity` conclusion; keep the table's original load/shape diagnostic and continue checks that do not depend on entity resolution (for example source-ID and timeline dependency checks). A successfully loaded empty or partial entity array remains assessable and must still produce genuine dangling findings. Apply consistently to relation endpoints, timeline entity/place references, and provenance holder lookups, or explicitly narrow and document any deferred branch. No need for generalized tri-state infrastructure or to suppress all downstream validation.

This is not “entity absent”: it is “entity-table lookup unavailable.” Conversely, loaded-and-missing ID is an input-table absence finding only, not proof of absence from the fictional world. The distinction should be visible in output or its documented interpretation; a silent suppression without preserving the loader diagnostic would obscure the reason checks were skipped.

## Falsifier and risks

Falsifier: matched controls show no dependent dangling cascade on the unload case, or suppression obscures findings that remain independently assessable / causes users to mistake an unavailable lookup for a valid reference. An explicit checker contract that intentionally defines failed tables as empty and treats these as useful partial-input diagnostics would also weaken the proposed calibration benefit. Test both unavailable and successfully loaded empty/partial controls; include missing and invalid/duplicate failure if policy is meant to cover them, and verify independent source/dependency errors survive. Compare all 99 old CLI tuples and 494 old fixture JSON bytes; any output delta beyond dependent lookup findings needs justification. Regression/cascade risk is suppression scope: over-broad early exits can hide unrelated findings, while partial suppression can leave other entity-dependent dangling messages misleading. Do not extend to source/timeline tables without their own dependency map and controls.
