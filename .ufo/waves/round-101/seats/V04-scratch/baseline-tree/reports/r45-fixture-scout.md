## Source wording (fictional input, not authentication)

Suggested new in-world report excerpt, cited by both events and faction claim:

> “At the third watch, the Reed Compact kept its harbor outpost staffed while its tower outpost remained staffed. The report records both posts under the Compact’s name during that watch.”

Use “third watch” only as a fixture-local label mapped to tick 3, not an absolute time. This wording supports the narrow authoring interpretation that the faction name denotes two simultaneous outposts, rather than one person physically present twice. It does **not** authenticate the fictional report, establish canon, or show that every faction or every timeline faction-row denotes distributed membership. The source ID must be unique and in-world; the README says source references are fictional records and not the separate real-world inspiration ledger (.shokunin/lore/corpus/fixtures/README.md:36-38). Do not label the prose a true/canonical fact.

## Risk and falsifiable recommendation

**Conditional go for a probe only; no implementation recommendation from fixture evidence alone.** The pair can falsify the narrow rule’s calibration premise: if the faction candidate is structurally valid and the checker flags it while the paired character conflict remains flagged, there is an observable differential case for independent review. But event `entity_id` might conventionally mean a single located entity/representative, and the invented source cannot establish that convention or world truth. Avoid broadening the exception to all noncharacters or treating a faction kind label as proof of multi-location. If the input convention cannot explicitly say these are outposts reported under the faction name—or the event is instead one singular faction entity—**no-go**: do not add this fixture or suppress the finding. Any narrowly faction-only exemption still risks silencing genuine conflicts for a faction modeled as one indivisible entity; require independent review and old-output replay before admission. Preserve character double-booking regardless.
