## Why this is a different note class from r15

- **r15 mark observation**: speaker reports a **present** sensory detail (blurred blue oval mark) on an object already in hand (the carbon), plus inability to name origin and explicit refusal to attribute the mark to the Blue Seal.
- **This proposal**: speaker reports a **negative finding** after a search — a named item was **not** at a named place. No mark, no color, no seal motif, no carbon re-inspection.
- Clause shape differs: r15 ≈ `saw(X) ∧ ¬identify(origin(X)) ∧ ¬attribute(X, BlueSeal)`; r23-class ≈ `searched_for(Y, locus) ∧ ¬found(Y, locus)`. Mapping this class is not a restatement of r15 rows or of r10 blank-destination text (blank field is omitted content on a kept page; absence-of-strip is missing object at a rack).
