# Round 14 critic — source backfill and depth challenge

## Observed facts

- The declaration in `.shokunin/lore/LOG.md` says the round-10 fixture already has a manually inspected `registrar-copy-excerpt.md`, but the `courier-log` and `archive-ledger` source IDs have metadata rows only; their cited claims cannot be compared against supplied source wording. The declared move is to add newly authored fictional excerpts and maps without changing lore JSON rows.
- At inspection, `.shokunin/lore/corpus/fixtures/r10-registry-witness/` contains `sources.json`, `entities.json`, `relations.json`, `timeline.json`, `contradictions.json`, and `registrar-copy-excerpt.md`; there is no courier or archive excerpt in that directory. `sources.json` describes courier-log as a fictional first-person personal log and archive-ledger as a fictional municipal record, with movements rather than omniscient truth. The existing registrar excerpt explicitly says it is invented fixture text, not recovered evidence.
- `entities.json` already asserts Mira carried the blue seal, Lantern Harbor has a registry, and North Tower has a watch bell. `relations.json` already asserts Mira carries the seal. `timeline.json` already asserts Mira departs the harbor at tick 1 and reaches the tower at tick 2; it also records Sella seeing the departure and leaving the destination blank at Mira's request. These are existing fixture rows, not evidence supplied by the metadata.
- The observed proposal in `.shokunin/lore/reports/r14-courier-scout.md` would phrase those already-present assertions as “At tick 1, I left Lantern Harbor. I carried the blue seal with me. At tick 2, I reached North Tower.” The proposal is explicitly not written to the fixture. This is a close restatement of the existing rows, not a newly evidenced route or event.

## Challenge

Backfilling an excerpt now under an already-cited source ID is retrospective authoring. It cannot independently corroborate the JSON rows: the excerpt is being composed to match them, and the mapping from row to phrase is authored by the same project that supplied the row. Labeling the excerpt “newly authored” and “not recovered evidence” is essential and honest, but does not remove that circular support. A source ID plus matching prose demonstrates internal consistency of a designed fixture, not that the source independently supports or discovered the claim. The archive-ledger metadata's stated limits also matter: it records movements, not omniscient truth; it cannot be made to establish more than a ledger entry would.

The courier proposal preserves the existing route endpoints, tick labels, carry assertion, and entity names. It adds no intermediate route, event, relationship, or uncertainty distinction. Thus, under a substantive meaning of depth (a richer world account or additional inspectable lore), the proposal alone does not establish a depth increase; it mostly documents the current claims in prose. A second file or a phrase-to-row map is not itself more lore. There is a narrower possible benefit: readers can inspect an authored in-world rendering of claims previously represented only as metadata/rows, and reviewers can detect wording mismatches. That is improved legibility/traceability of the fixture, not independent evidentiary support, and should not be conflated with added canon or corroboration.

The map is also not verification: matching a sentence to an existing row proves only that the draft was tailored to that row. “First appearance” is an index assignment, not something a single excerpt can prove to be the character's first appearance in all possible records. First-person “I” plus the source title is attribution inside the fiction, not identity verification. Likewise, “with me” can support accompaniment in this invented text, but not ownership, origin, or custody beyond the bounded wording.

## Recommendation (not a fact or verdict)

- Keep each excerpt explicitly marked as newly authored fictional fixture text, not recovered evidence; state that its relation to the existing source ID is a fixture convention. Do not describe the new text as an independent source, validation, or recovered witness.
- Publish the maps as editorial traceability aids, not proof that source text validates claims. Distinguish “phrase restates row” from “new lore detail.” If all JSON remains unchanged and the excerpts only restate rows as proposed, describe the move conservatively as documentation/legibility. Do not claim substantive depth improved solely because there are additional files.
- If the intended axis is specifically depth, identify a concrete new, bounded fictional detail or meaningful qualification that deepens the account, with its authored status and source limitations explicit; do not silently add it to canon or the JSON. Conversely, adding details solely to satisfy the axis risks expanding the world without need. The adjudicator should be free to conclude depth unchanged or unprovable, rather than counting prose volume.
- For archive wording, do not erase the existing blank-destination limitation or imply the ledger reveals Mira's destination. The source metadata explicitly limits it to recording movements, not omniscient truth; the registrar excerpt's blank field is part of the fixture's stated uncertainty.

This critique concerns only the observed baseline files and the courier scout's uncommitted proposal. It does not assert that either proposed excerpt was implemented, that the fictional claims are true, or that the round should be admitted.
