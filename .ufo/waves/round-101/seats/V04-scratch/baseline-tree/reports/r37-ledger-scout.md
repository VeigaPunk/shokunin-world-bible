## Risks / bounds

A repeated event ID makes the ledger endpoint ambiguous as a unique structured assertion, even though a last row remains in the internal map. Do not select first or last as the intended claim, treat the two accounts as one, or call either account false. This is a structural pointer failure, not a finding about testimony, shared occasion, semantic incompatibility, truth, or canon. The prose rationale (`r35-bell7-conflict/contradictions.json:11`) remains editorial; an ID match and checker output cannot authenticate its assumptions. A duplicate elsewhere in timeline can produce its own findings, so report the complete checker output rather than predicting only the endpoint error.
