I assess a **small, conditional incremental calibration gain**, not a new lore or semantic gain. A reader opening the r43 Jorin map alone encounters an unqualified present-tense “is stale” characterization of a map whose current content is corrected. Time-bounding this one residual cross-reference could prevent that narrow reader from distrusting the corrected local map or confusing the accurate r43-at-creation record with current state. But the README already routes the operator correctly, and r48’s repaired Vela map itself states the current correction. Hence the gain is less than r50’s active README fix and may be mere repeated polish rather than material improvement. No observed reader study establishes that users actually follow this route.

Any proposed revision must retain that the inherited map truly was stale at r43 creation and must not rewrite historical reports.
