## Novelty, semantics, and materiality boundary

Novelty is limited to the **validity gate**, not the same-reference concept or its current behavior. Current behavior clearly diagnoses matching raw values even after side-specific field errors. Whether that is useful redundancy or an unsupported duplicate-reference assertion is the Round 69 calibration question. A malformed side does still have a raw dictionary/value, and exact equality of those raw values is observable; but it is not a structurally valid `(event_id, field)` pair under the checker contract. The r62 report does not resolve which interpretation should govern.

A fair matched experiment should preserve four distinctions from the declaration: malformed-both identical (including absent/malformed components), malformed-one, valid-identical, and valid-different. It should compare full outputs and exits, not only the same-reference line; ensure per-side malformed diagnostics remain, and ensure valid identical pairs continue to get the finding while valid different pairs do not. The specific malformed-both fixture is already present in r62, so it cannot be presented as a newly discovered behavior case. Evidence that suppressing the extra finding improves repair guidance rather than merely reducing one line is still needed; equally, evidence that the finding is an independently useful pointer on malformed sides would falsify the proposed gain.

No same-reference finding here establishes a fictional contradiction: `claim_a`/`claim_b` are input-ledger pointers, and malformed field values do not identify a usable assertion pair. No inference about source text, canon, rights, or game mechanics is made. I did not run checker probes, inspect source prose, or assess operator response.
