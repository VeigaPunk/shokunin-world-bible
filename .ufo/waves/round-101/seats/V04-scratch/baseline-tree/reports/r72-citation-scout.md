## Required in-world citation audit for a candidate

The sole permitted new world-bible fact is that **Vela reports** a close reading and a tentative maker inference. It should be attributed in the new excerpt, represented in one new claim on the existing `vela` entity, and cite a distinct new source ID in both the source row and claim. The source row should describe a newly authored, fictional, unverified first-person report—not an authenticated slate or author. Do not cite inherited `vela-note` or `jorin-note` for the new slate assertion: their described contents do not support it. Retain the existing `vela` entity and its inherited claims unchanged; do not create an entity for a slate or maker, since that exceeds the declared one-claim-only design and would risk implying their existence/identity as established facts.

The clause map should quote the new excerpt literally and point each factual clause to the exact new claim field and its `source_id`. Separate (1) what Vela says she could read from (2) what she explicitly says she infers; do not let the claim convert her inference into observed or settled authorship. Include any uncertainty/knowledge limit only if the excerpt actually states it. A source pointer establishes an in-world attribution link, not that the report is true or that its wording is independently corroborated. The map is editorial evidence; it does not repair unsupported or over-broad claim wording.

The r72 design deliberately adds no timeline event, so there is no event citation to check or occasion/tick to assign. Do not imply that this report precedes, follows, coincides with, or concerns the same authenticated slate/occasion as inherited events. Avoid wording that implies verified artifact existence, writer, placement, handwriting, seal/Blue Seal provenance, intent, or canon. No contradiction is resolved. These are attribution limits, not claims about the world beyond the fixture.
