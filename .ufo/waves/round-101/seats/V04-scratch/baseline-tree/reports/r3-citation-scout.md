# Round 3 citation scout — ledger assertions and canon caution

## Evidence in the existing corpus

- The round-3 claim is specifically that each explicit contradiction-ledger entry will contain `claim_a`, `claim_b`, and nonempty `why`, and that both claim references resolve to cited claims. It says the ledger is editorial evidence, not semantic proof or game-mechanics verification. See `.shokunin/lore/LOG.md`, “Round 3 — 2026-10-12 (depth axis-move; declaration before dispatch)”.
- The established citation pattern is an in-world source row in `sources.json`, identified by `id`, with `title`, `kind`, and `reliability_note`; examples are `archive-ledger` and `courier-log` in `.shokunin/lore/corpus/fixtures/contradictions/sources.json`. Entities have claim-granular `{statement, source_id}` and cite `first_appearance`; relations and timeline events each cite their own `source_id` (`.shokunin/lore/corpus/fixtures/contradictions/entities.json`, `relations.json`, and `timeline.json`).
- All three currently seeded checker findings have sourced underlying rows: the dangling `consults` relation cites `courier-log`; the `arrival` event cites `courier-log` and the competing `false-sighting` cites `archive-ledger`; `arrival`'s dependency inversion is also attached to that sourced event. See `.shokunin/lore/corpus/fixtures/contradictions/relations.json` and `timeline.json`. These are fixture records, not externally established story canon.
- The checker resolves source IDs against `sources.json`, but does not inspect whether the source's contents support a statement or judge its truth: `.shokunin/lore/corpus/fixtures/check_bible.py` (`source()` and entity/relation/timeline checks). The fixture README explicitly says a citation is not proof the source is omniscient and checker output does not choose canon: `.shokunin/lore/corpus/fixtures/README.md`.
- Real-world method citations are deliberately separate from in-world story evidence. `.shokunin/lore/corpus/knowledge/reference-ledger.md` records attribution, rights, and AI-training permission questions separately and says method references do not establish story facts. The decision table calls the source-ID requirement a local editorial rule, not a PROV-O requirement: `.shokunin/lore/corpus/knowledge/2026-10-12-lore-decision-table.md`.

## Recommendations (not existing behavior)

1. Make each ledger side resolve to an actual claim assertion, not just a source ID or free-floating paraphrase. The round-3 declaration requires resolvable cited claims; the current schema has no claim IDs or contradiction ledger, so the exact reference encoding remains undecided. A stable claim key or an unambiguous entity/claim locator would avoid accidentally citing an unrelated assertion.
2. Retain citation at assertion granularity: each side should carry or resolve to its own `source_id`, which must resolve to an in-world record. Do not treat the contradiction entry's `why` as a substitute for evidence, or one side's citation as support for both sides. The existing data convention supports per-claim citations; it does not yet implement these contradiction-ledger checks.
3. Treat `why` as an editorial explanation of the incompatibility, not as an assertion that either source is right. Preserve both sides and report the conflict for human editorial review; do not rank sources, infer a winner from reliability notes, or rewrite the underlying entities/events. This follows the round-3 no-selection claim and the existing README/decision-table boundary.
4. Avoid false canon claims by labeling these records as invented examples, not importing their names/events into project canon, and by omitting unsupported assertions rather than manufacturing a source. The fixture README says all its entries are invented and uncertain claims should be omitted; `.shokunin/lore/corpus/knowledge/seed.md` likewise says unsupported assertions must not be presented as canon.
5. State checker limits whenever reporting a pass: resolvable citation proves only a pointer exists, not source authenticity, textual support, reliability, corroboration, or semantic truth. Do not cite `.shokunin/lore/corpus/knowledge/reference-ledger.md` method references as in-world evidence.

## Boundary

These are evidence and recommendations for ledger design only. The current checker validates source-ID resolution, not truth or textual entailment, and the current contradiction fixture has no explicit `claim_a`/`claim_b`/`why` ledger. No checker, fixture, or shared documentation was changed for this scout task.
