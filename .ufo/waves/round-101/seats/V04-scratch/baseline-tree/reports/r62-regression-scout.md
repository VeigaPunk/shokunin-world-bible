**Proposed, not observed:** retain the timeline loader error, and withhold only ledger event-ID existence/cardinality assertions when the whole timeline table failed to load. Continue ledger object/side/event_id/field shape and claim-reference-distinctness validation, plus all checks over independently loaded tables. For a successfully loaded empty or partial timeline, preserve current unknown-ID results; for loaded duplicate event IDs, preserve cardinality/ambiguity results. Do not claim that invalid/unavailable means empty, or generalize the gate to dependencies (no timeline rows are available there to traverse).

A discriminating temporary matrix should use identical other four tables and ledger bytes: malformed/missing/duplicate-member/non-array timeline; loaded `[]`; loaded partial with one referenced ID present and one absent; and loaded duplicate ID. Record complete stdout/stderr/exit. Include malformed ledger pointer shape/field and a separate relation/source fault to prove independent diagnostics survive. The proposed win exists only if failed timeline no longer presents `expected exactly one ...` as a known absence, while the primary loader error and independent checks remain, and loaded empty/partial/duplicate behavior stays intact. If the primary error already conveys uncertainty sufficiently and removal of location-specific pointers materially impairs repair, that is a real counterweight; record costs, do not assume suppression is beneficial. A change that suppresses loaded-empty findings, masks malformed ledger data, or changes unrelated diagnostics falsifies the narrow policy.

## Costs and boundary

Withholding a ledger-local path loses a convenient pointer to the affected `claim_a`/`claim_b`; the file-level load error is actionable, and rerun after repair may restore membership checks, but this does not erase the present triage cost. Conversely, current `expected exactly one timeline event` may overstate a conclusion when no index loaded. Paired outputs and reviewer judgment are needed to determine materiality, rather than borrowing the r60/r61 admission.
