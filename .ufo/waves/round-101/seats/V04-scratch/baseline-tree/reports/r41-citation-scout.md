## Boundaries and risks

1. **Do not infer an objective bell event.** If the changed excerpt is authored as proposed, it attributes a stronger assertion/identification to Vela. It still does not independently establish an actual registry bell, the sound’s source, an objective ringing, or an authenticated Bell-7 chronology. The existing Bell-7/date wording remains Vela’s fictional occasion claim; tick 3 remains fixture-local, not synchronization (`.shokunin/lore/LOG.md:663, 676, 680`; `timeline.json:41-47`).
2. **Describe the fixed-row discrepancy precisely.** The unchanged claim/event preserves Vela’s tentative “took to be” formulation. Relative to the proposed excerpt, it is a less strong rendition and may omit the excerpt’s direct identification; a reviewer can find it non-exact or under-specified. Do not say the rows are necessarily false or logically incompatible: a weaker report can be compatible with a stronger one. The calibration question is whether the reviewer notices the strength difference and refuses to treat citation-ID resolution or checker success as proof that the record exactly captures the excerpt.
3. **No retrofit or canon choice.** Neither the stronger prose nor its source pointer selects between Tarin’s Harbor placement report and Sella’s Tower account, validates Vela as corroboration, resolves their conditional ledger, or proves Sella/envelope absent. Vela’s unchanged non-sighting is only her report of not seeing them (`entities.json:79-83`; `timeline.json:45-47`; r40 adjudication summary `.shokunin/lore/LOG.md:674-676`).
4. **No rights inference.** A fictional citation, excerpt label, or checker pass does not establish copyright ownership, license, quotation/reuse authority, or AI-training permission; the cited rights ledger leaves those questions unassessed (`reference-ledger.md:5-7`).

## Falsifiable check

Compare the exact r40 and proposed r41 Vela excerpts against the unchanged `vela` claim and `vela-listening-report` event, including their cited `vela-note` source. Pass the narrow calibration check only if the reviewer identifies that r41’s prose directly attributes the bell identification to Vela whereas both fixed rows retain her tentative “took to be” wording, calls this a source-to-record strength/precision mismatch (or omission), and explicitly leaves open whether the weaker rows are logically compatible rather than labeling them false. Also require the reviewer not to promote the excerpt into objective bell origin/ringing, objective absence, corroboration, chronology, or canon, and not to infer real-world rights or training permission. Fail if the reviewer treats the source ID/clean checker as semantic validation, misses the wording difference, claims the weak rows prove the excerpt false, or asserts an objective bell event or rights clearance. This check is falsified if the actual r41 excerpt does not make the stipulated wording change, any JSON row changes, or the reviewer makes none of the stated bounded distinctions.
