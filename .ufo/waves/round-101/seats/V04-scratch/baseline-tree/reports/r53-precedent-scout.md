Do not say the strip was actually in her possession, remained in her satchel, was the same strip Sella sought, or was absent from the rack; do not infer theft, cause, shared occasion, or timeline beyond what the authored clause supports. A tick placed between existing events is fixture ordering only, not an independent date or duration. No carbon/Blue Seal/mark link, objective fact, canon, or rights claim.

Falsifier for a future go: a non-author must be able to point to exact clauses establishing a materially informative, conditional relation between the two reports that survives removal of any assumed same-strip identity or unsupported timing. If removing those assumptions leaves only “Mira says she handled a strip” plus “Sella reports not finding a strip,” or if JSON expresses more than the attributed clauses, depth remains unchanged and the candidate should be rejected. No checker behavior can establish prose entailment or custody.
