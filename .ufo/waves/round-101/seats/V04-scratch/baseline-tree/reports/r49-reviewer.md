# Round 49 independent reviewer — Sella/Jorin handoff

## Verdict

**NO-GO on the declared depth gain. NO-GO on an unqualified `to_holder: jorin` provenance edge.** The edge would overstate what the proposed evidence can establish: at most, an invented first-person Sella note could say that she handed the same carbon to someone she identifies as Jorin. It cannot establish Jorin's actual receipt or subsequent custody. The existing provenance schema has no source-attribution/claim-status field, and a source citation or CLEAN structural result does not qualify the row.

## Independent assessment

R10's invented registry excerpt has Sella say she left the destination blank and “kept the carbon.” R15's separately labeled, newly authored fictional follow-up has Sella say she looked again at “the carbon I had kept,” saw a blurred blue oval, could not identify its origin, and did not attribute it to Mira's Blue Seal. These establish the connected retained-carbon and later-inspection reports, not a transfer or independent authentication of a persistent physical object's identity.

A new Sella-only note explicitly saying “I handed the carbon I kept to Jorin” would be a more precise attribution of an additional reported disposition, and could give the reader a claimed recipient. But as currently declared it adds one assertion and one recipient; it supplies no consequence, second perspective, corroboration, or new uncertainty-bearing discovery. R6 already supplies generic provenance mechanics; r10/r15 already connect Sella, the retained carbon, and its later inspection. Thus the proposed note alone does not materially deepen the connected story beyond adding that assertion/recipient. Treating the handoff as accomplished custody via an unqualified chain would make the apparent gain depend on an evidentiary overstatement.

## Limits and falsifier

If this idea is revisited, keep the handoff solely at the level of Sella's explicitly attributed report; do not use an unqualified `to_holder: jorin` edge or claim Jorin received, possessed, acknowledged, or retained the carbon. “The same carbon” and Jorin's identity/office remain Sella's claims, not independently authenticated facts. Do not infer any clock time, destination, Bell-7 synchronization, or any connection between the blurred oval and Mira's Blue Seal; no conclusion about canon or rights follows.

A falsifier that could change the depth judgment would be a specific, connected narrative consequence or genuinely distinct perspective in the proposed material, identified in exact source clauses and found by a non-author review to add more than the handoff allegation/recipient. An independent Jorin account could support a separately attributed receipt claim, but would not by itself authenticate real-world custody or establish material depth. Until that evidence exists, no fixture is warranted and no handoff note or chain should be represented as built or validated.
