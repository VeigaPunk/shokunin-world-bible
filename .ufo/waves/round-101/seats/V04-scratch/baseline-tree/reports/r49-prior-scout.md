# Round 49 prior-art scout — carbon handoff depth

## Finding

**Conditional no-go on the proposed handoff as written; depth not established.** An authored Sella note naming Jorin can add a new attributed allegation, but a reported handoff is not a receipt, and the “same carbon” bridge is asserted by the author rather than independently evidenced. A provenance chain would encode this allegation, not authenticate it. A genuinely distinct scene/account could be narratively new, but adding chain rows or another witness voice alone is not material depth.

## Risks / boundaries

The proposed note may claim Sella handed a carbon to Jorin, but cannot establish that transfer happened, that Jorin received or acknowledged it, that the carbon is objectively the same physical object, or that Jorin’s receipt exists. Naming the carbon as “the carbon I kept” is source attribution; encoding it as one item with successive holders gives the model a continuity assertion, not independent identity evidence. Do not turn an unsupported claim into a confirmed provenance fact, invent Jorin’s independent testimony, or infer the oval’s identity from its color/shape. Nothing here establishes canon, external source authentication, or real-world rights/permission.

## Falsifiable decision

**No-go for depth admission** if the only new content is Sella’s newly authored handoff assertion plus a structurally valid provenance edge: r6 already supplies the mechanics, while r10/r15 supply the retained carbon and follow-up, and the edge’s added continuity is solely the new author’s claim. Reconsider only if the proposed source contains a distinct, specific narrative consequence or genuinely new perspective beyond “Sella says she handed it over,” explicitly keeps recipient/identity at the level of report, and a non-author reviewer can point to that clause-level novelty without relying on row count or treating provenance validation as authentication. Falsify depth if removing the handoff rows leaves no new story information, or if the reviewer must infer Jorin’s receipt, objective same-object continuity, mark identity, or canon. Distinct depth assessment: **not demonstrated by the declaration**; no fixture or checker was inspected as a candidate and no result is implied.
