# Round 55 simplifier — shared-rack comparison

## Decision: NO-GO on material depth (before build)

The proposed direct shared-rack comparison is sharper than r53’s satchel-handling note, but still does not clear the materiality bar. A hypothetical Mira report of placing “my outbound route strip” on the Lantern Harbor registry hook rack would overlap Sella’s reported search at that rack. That yields a narrowly comparable pair of attributed statements; it does not establish same physical strip, compatible occasion, duration or intervening state. Without those, there is no consequential discrepancy or explanation—only a conditional question whose useful content depends on unresolved identity and sequence. Do not build on this proposal absent independent evidence of a reader-relevant distinction that does not smuggle those premises in.

The simplest imaginable comparison is just: Mira reports placement at the rack; Sella later reports non-finding at the rack. It might prompt “how do these reports relate?” only conditional on same-strip identity and compatible occasions. No strip identifier or bounded interval in the described text establishes either. The rack overlap improves the location match over r53, where Mira’s proposed satchel note lacked direct overlap; r53 rejected materiality because same strip and compatible sequencing were unestablished (`.shokunin/lore/reports/r53-simplifier.md:5-9`). But a better overlap is not itself a material interpretive consequence.

R43 is not a warrant to admit: its narrow useful comparison was two attributed reports locating the same named person at the same specific place, while expressly not proving a shared Bell-7 occasion (`.shokunin/lore/reports/r43-simplifier.md:5-15`). Here the supposed object’s physical identity and timing matter to the comparison itself, not merely to stronger corroboration. R30 likewise cautions that textual conflict depends on proposition alignment and does not establish canon truth (`.shokunin/lore/reports/r30-triad-scout.md:1-3,14-18`).

## No-go / falsifier

Remain NO-GO unless a non-author reviewer can state a specific useful distinction gained from the rack placement vs non-finding that survives both (a) uncertainty whether “my outbound route strip” and “Mira’s outbound route strip” mean the same physical item and (b) uncertainty about compatible sequence. Reject if the value is only “there is a question,” if the proposed account relies on an unstated interval/continuous presence, or if it implies placement was witnessed/verified. Reconsider only with a source-supported, independently reviewed material comparison—not merely more elaborate mapping. If later built, the checker could validate structure/references only, not prose truth or this materiality judgment.

Attribution boundary: any Mira sentence is hypothetical newly authored fiction, not recovered evidence. These reports do not establish physical strip identity, continuous rack presence, removal/theft, objective absence or an actual contradiction. No canon, rights/permission, Blue Seal or carbon link is supported.
