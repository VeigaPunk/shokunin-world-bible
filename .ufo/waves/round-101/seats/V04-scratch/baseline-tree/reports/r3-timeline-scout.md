# Round 3 scout: mapping the three seeded violations

## Evidence examined

The original contradictory fixture is `.shokunin/lore/corpus/fixtures/contradictions/`. Its source index is `sources.json`: `courier-log` is “Courier Mira’s Route Log, year 12” (fictional personal log), and `archive-ledger` is “Lantern Harbor Registry, year 12” (fictional municipal record). Both reliability notes explicitly say they are in-world fiction and not omniscient truth. The checker is `.shokunin/lore/corpus/fixtures/check_bible.py`; a fresh run reports exactly three violations. The records below are the raw cited evidence, not adjudications about which account is true.

## Recommendation (not additional evidence)

For the first two rows, an explicit contradiction ledger could point at the existing record locations and retain both source IDs: `timeline[1]` versus `timeline[3]` for the dependency inversion, and `timeline[1]` versus `timeline[2]` for the same-tick location conflict. Keep the exact claims/descriptions, source references, and explanation visible; the ledger should be editorial metadata, not a semantic proof or resolution.

Do not model the dangling endpoint as two cited claims. If the round requires every ledger row to have two resolvable cited claim references, the existing dangling-endpoint diagnostic cannot honestly satisfy that requirement. Either leave it outside that claim-pair ledger, or use an explicitly different malformed-reference diagnostic form with the missing side labeled absent/unresolvable and no fabricated entity/source. The latter is a design recommendation only; no fixture or checker changes were made in this scout lane.
