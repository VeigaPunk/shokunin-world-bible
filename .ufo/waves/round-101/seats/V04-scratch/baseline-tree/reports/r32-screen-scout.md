## Risks / interpretation boundary

A blank title should cause the required-field finding and exclude that row under the shared admission code; it should not be counted as a second admitted `courier-log`. The absent duplicate/ambiguous-reference findings would therefore describe this checker's admitted-source index only. They do not show that the raw row is absent, that either in-world record is false or trustworthy, that a citation is supported by source text, that a source is unique in the fictional world, or that any real-world rights or AI-training permission exists. Keep the comparison to the valid r31 duplicate and unique control; do not interpret this as canon or source-quality judgment. A key attribution risk is altering any additional source field or any referencing table while making the probe, which would confound the result. The path supplied in the round declaration is a fixture path, not evidence that the fixture has been implemented or run yet.
