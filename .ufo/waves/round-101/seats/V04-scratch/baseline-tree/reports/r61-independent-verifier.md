## Attribution and boundaries
The checker tracks successful load of source and entity tables separately from their row contents: empty arrays count as available; malformed, unreadable, non-array, or duplicate-member JSON does not. This accounts for unknown/ambiguous source reporting and availability-sensitive checks. Provenance-chain checking requires available sources and a unique item entity; unknown/unavailable citations break chain validation, whereas ambiguous citations are flagged and subsequent chain diagnostics are marked “input-structural finding, not sourced-canon.” The r39 loaded-but-missing-source and r5 cases were not independently attributed from the fixture names alone; no source/canon conclusion is made.
