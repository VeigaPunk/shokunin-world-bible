The candidate is an independent attributed observation of Vela's reported location, not corroboration of the bell or a synchronized event. The round declaration proposes Jorin, from a registry doorway, seeing Vela on the Lantern Harbor east steps “during what Jorin called Bell-7” and expressly not seeing who struck any bell (`.shokunin/lore/LOG.md:718`). The wording supports only a conditional overlap: Jorin reports Vela at a location, while Vela reports standing there (`r40-vela-listener/vela-note-excerpt.md:1`; `r41-vela-direct-bell/vela-note-excerpt.md:1`). It does not verify their reports independently of their authored fixture texts, establish that both use Bell-7 to mean the same actual occasion, or show they observed/heard the same strokes.

R40's excerpt is Vela's own reported position and sensory account, including tentative identification (“I took to be”) and non-sighting (`r40-vela-listener/vela-note-excerpt.md:1`). Jorin's second authored account cannot repair that mismatch or establish an objective bell event.

The occasion language is especially weak as synchronization evidence. In r35, Tarin and Sella independently *claim* incompatible places during Bell-7, while the manual map warns that matching local tick 3 is editorial sequence metadata, not proof of simultaneity (`r35-bell7-conflict/tarin-note-excerpt.md:1`; `r35-bell7-conflict/sella-bell7-note-excerpt.md:1`; `r35-bell7-conflict/manual-clause-pointer-map.md`, Tarin event row). Jorin's phrase “what Jorin called Bell-7” is even explicitly a naming attribution, not a bridge proving shared occasion.

## Falsification risks

- **False corroboration:** matching place reports provide conditional, cross-account consistency only if Jorin's and Vela's identities, place references, and source claims are taken as referring to the same Lantern Harbor east steps. The accounts are newly authored fiction, not independent external evidence. A clean structural checker cannot authenticate either.
- **Shared-occasion laundering:** common label Bell-7, event tick, or narrative proximity does not establish same actual bell sounding, date, time, or simultaneity. Jorin expressly does not see a striker; neither that omission nor Vela's auditory report verifies objective ringing.
- **Claim inflation:** Jorin's observation cannot corroborate Vela hearing strokes, her bell identification, the bell's origin, or the reported Bell-7 chronology. Nor does it resolve Tarin/Sella's incompatible attributed reports.
- **Absence inflation:** Vela's “did not see Sella or an envelope” is a bounded non-sighting (`r40-vela-listener/vela-note-excerpt.md:1`; `r41-vela-direct-bell/vela-note-excerpt.md:1`), not evidence either was absent. Jorin's position/observation cannot establish their absence either.
- **Novelty/depth risk:** a further witness and another location assertion may be row-count expansion rather than a new narrative capability. R10 already has a registrar's attributed observation/record about Mira at Harbor (`r10-registry-witness/registrar-copy-excerpt.md:5`); r33 has Tarin's first-person direct observation of Sella placing an envelope at the Harbor desk (`r33-tarin-envelope/tarin-note-excerpt.md:1`). R40 already contributes a distinct Vela-reported position and auditory/non-sighting perspective. Jorin adds a potentially useful *cross-account comparison* of a person's reported location, but not a new sensory or occasion-verification class. Depth gain is therefore modest and unproven, not automatic.

## Falsifiable check

Read the exact proposed Jorin excerpt and every new claim/event field side by side with Vela's exact r40/r41 excerpts. Mark each proposition as directly attributed, inferred, or unsupported. The test passes narrowly only if (1) Jorin explicitly reports seeing Vela at the east steps; (2) the record preserves Jorin's attribution and limited vantage, rather than asserting an objective event; (3) Vela's location is described as *her report*, with no claim Jorin proves her truth; and (4) Bell-7 is kept as Jorin's label/source-claimed occasion, with no asserted shared sounding, objective bell strike, date/time synchronization, Sella/envelope absence, or resolution of the existing ledger. Fail if any omitted observer, object, event, or occasion is silently supplied by matching IDs/ticks or by the fixture's own prose. For depth, ask whether the paired accounts let a reader inspect a meaningful cross-report relation that r10/r33/r40 could not already express; if the only delta is another name and a place row, classify depth unchanged.

## Verdict

**Conditional go for the narrow artifact experiment; no-go for any claim of verified shared occasion or objective corroboration.** The defensible finding is only that Jorin's independently attributed sighting *conditionally corroborates Vela's reported east-steps location*, assuming both accounts refer to the same named place. The declaration itself flags that the occasion remains unverified (`.shokunin/lore/LOG.md:718`). Depth novelty is modest and presently **unproven**: r10/r33 precedent and r40's existing second perspective make a new witness insufficient by itself. Admit a depth gain only if the delivered text/record creates a genuinely inspectable cross-account relation under the bounded test above; otherwise depth unchanged. No inference establishes objective bell ringing, exact shared occasion, Sella/envelope absence, Bell-7 chronology, canon, or real-world rights.
