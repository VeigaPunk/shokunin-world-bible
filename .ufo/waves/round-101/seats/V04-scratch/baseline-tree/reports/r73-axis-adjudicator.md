The new clause map reproduces the exact spans, including the nested inner quotation marks. The critic's arbitrariness objection remains real: an author can invent an unlike slogan at will, and Jorin's inherited Bell-7 sighting of Vela does **not** place him at her slate-reading or show that he saw her read. Nor does the new copying assignment prove who sent him or establish a shared notice. Still, `OPEN` versus `CLEAR` is a concrete difference between *reported gate directives*, rather than generic scene decoration or duplicate testimony; the copying assignment gives Jorin a stated reason to attend to precise words. Juxtaposing that limited report with Vela's close-reading account creates a modest perspective/open-question gain. The two directives can coexist on different objects or occasions. The gain is in the attributed discrepancy, **not** a mystery about one proven persistent inscription, a verified conflict, or corroboration. The comparison note is necessary to make this modest gain legible; without its unresolved-identity/time caveat, the same prose would invite an unsupported inference.
