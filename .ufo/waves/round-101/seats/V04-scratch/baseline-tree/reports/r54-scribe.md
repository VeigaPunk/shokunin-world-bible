## Judgment

**Material depth: NO-GO on the available evidence.** If authored exactly as proposed, Jorin would be a distinct fictional reporter and a distinct reported visual observation. But the comparison’s apparent significance—that another observer saw Sella’s oval on the retained carbon—depends on unverified sheet identity and mark continuity. The phrase “what she called the carbon she kept” preserves a claim about Sella’s identification, not physical identity. Without those links, the addition is another attributed observation of a similar-looking mark, not a demonstrated new cross-account consequence. It risks manufactured corroboration if presented as confirmation of r15.

This is consistent with `.shokunin/lore/reports/r49-scribe.md` (“Depth: not yet demonstrated / conditional”; handoff could only be Sella’s fictional report, not authenticated transfer or Jorin’s receipt) and `.shokunin/lore/reports/r43-scribe.md` (“Depth novelty assessment”: a separate Jorin-reported sighting is useful only conditionally, and novelty fails if merely another disconnected witness/row). The r43 analogy does not establish this candidate’s materiality; here the observed sheet and mark are not independently linked to the prior account.

**Coverage: unchanged. Calibration: unchanged.** This is not a checker-rule test or demonstrated improvement in reader calibration. No candidate or baseline replay is claimed.

**Assumptions:** I treat Round 54’s proposed wording as hypothetical and the inherited excerpts as fictional text exactly as printed. I assume no unstated evidence connects Jorin’s sheet to Sella’s physical carbon or aligns the observation times.

**Falsifier / reconsideration condition:** A bounded, independently reviewed account could change this judgment if authored source text and a non-author’s clause map establish a meaningful, explicit relation between the object/mark Jorin saw and Sella’s reported observation without relying on source-ID, schema, or checker status. Merely repeating “the carbon she kept,” naming the oval, or adding rows would not suffice. Any representation must continue to distinguish report from fact and must not imply actual custody, Blue Seal identity, canon, or rights.
