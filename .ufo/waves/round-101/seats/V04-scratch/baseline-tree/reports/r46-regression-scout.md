The r43 source says “I saw Vela standing on the Lantern Harbor east steps” (`.shokunin/lore/corpus/fixtures/r43-jorin-vela-sighting/jorin-note-excerpt.md:1`). Its entity statement says Jorin “reports seeing Vela” (`.../entities.json:93`); its event says “Jorin reports seeing Vela” (`.../timeline.json:55`). The map characterizes this as a claimed visual sighting and says overlap with Vela’s account is conditional on same place and occasion (`.../jorin-clause-map.md:7`), while Bell-7 is only Jorin’s label (`.../jorin-clause-map.md:8`).

The proposed one-clause change—“I saw someone I took to be Vela”—makes the *person referent* uncertain. The fixed record’s definite Vela identification may still be logically compatible (perhaps the person was Vela), but the altered text no longer directly identifies the person as Vela; it reports Jorin’s tentative identification. This is a source-to-record precision/support gap, not a proof that the record is false or that the person was not Vela. Do not call it an objective non-Vela case, a shared occasion, or canon. Do not convert Jorin’s vantage/source claim into verification of Vela’s location or her own account.

Novelty is a real risk, not a presumption. R34 contrasts direct seeing with “heard that” hearsay and explicitly avoids inferring event truth (`.shokunin/lore/corpus/fixtures/r34-tarin-hearsay/modality-contrast-map.md:3,8,14`). The proposed variation keeps visual seeing but weakens who Jorin identifies; this is a distinct referent-identity dimension, yet remains another source/record precision mismatch. More seriously, r41 already establishes a confidence-strength precedent: tentative “I took to be” versus direct identification, where fixed JSON remains logically compatible but under-specific (`.shokunin/lore/corpus/fixtures/r41-vela-direct-bell/strength-contrast-map.md:3,5`). Reverse direction and a new entity type alone may not establish meaningful novelty. ## Risks / controls

- **Overclaim:** the fixed Jorin JSON might be compatible with the tentative wording; report only that it overstates the excerpt’s direct identification / precision. Do not label it logically incompatible or conclusively unsupported in every possible world.
- **Identity ambiguity:** no objective evidence establishes who the person was. The test concerns textual strength, not actual identity.
- **Account linking:** the excerpt does not prove the same occasion as Vela’s report; matching “Bell-7” does not establish shared occurrence (`jorin-clause-map.md:8,12`).
- **Checker scope:** JSON-only structural success says nothing about source entailment. Avoid presenting exit 0 as a prose judgment.
- **Confounding:** change only the quoted source clause in a clone; retain all five JSON tables unchanged and record exact diff. Do not alter inherited maps/tables or quietly repair the definite wording.
