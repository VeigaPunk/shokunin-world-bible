## Findings & named debt

- The unpatched checker already emits the raw-present FN-1 wording and the three-cause joined summary; these fixtures pin those outputs durably. Raw-present rows are also visibly schema-invalid, so their schema errors remain in the expected output.
- The fixtures were not replayed against X1's integrated checker; that is a phase-2 check. They do not measure the 160-tuple census or the C1 hedging deltas.
- Wait protocol: the first one-call poll used a 60-second cadence and 20-minute deadline. Its `X1-patch` label came from a broad `patches/X1-*` glob, so 05:56:55 only proves an X1-named artifact was present; `B1.md` first appeared at 06:00:55.
