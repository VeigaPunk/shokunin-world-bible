## Findings & named debt

- **M1-D1 — denominator mismatch:** this mission's requested 22 rows are 14 S2 JSON facts plus 8 hymn clauses. The broader r89 D1 denominator is also 22 but means 7 `first_appearance` pointers + 7 entity claims + 4 relations + 4 events. S2's 14-row set co-maps six pointers and excludes the hymn-sourced `entities[2].claims[0]`; the map explicitly crosswalks that claim and the remaining pointer to H4, without silently changing the requested 22-row tally. Integration should preserve the distinction between these two denominators.
- **R3:** the charter record attests a reciprocal pledge, not performance. The line and map preserve this boundary; `keeps_watch_with` must not be read as proof the factions performed the watch.
- **T3/T4:** the gate slip and register place one same vow at incompatible locations. Both remain attributed, unverified and unresolved; no winner is selected.
- The structural checker does not read the map or verify prose/R9 fidelity. M1 supplies the map and excerpt-level mode labels only; M3's reproducible support command and enforcement are separate C2 work.
- **S2 format dependency absent:** the 20 timestamped 60-second checks all returned `present=no` (05:51:51–06:10:51); an additional final check at 06:13:06 also returned `present=no`. The poll loop ended at check 20 (19 minutes after check 1), so the exact 20-minute boundary was not sampled; the later final check confirms no artifact had landed by then. The map therefore follows the available r89 S2 fact-map shape and the persisted R9 rule.
