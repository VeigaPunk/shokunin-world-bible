H3 (round 88) re-asks r87 H3's question of the delivered r88 checker: after C1 landed
(arm-from-shape-invalid + first-cause tracking + rewording), is every defect in the r87
mutation set either **reported** or **explicitly disclosed-as-unassessed**, with none

  `.ufo/waves/round-87/followup/H3-mutation-delivered.json` and the exact lambdas from

  `armed-record-tick-inversion` and `armed-record-holder-swap` (mutate the armed
  shape-invalid+ambiguous record of `r88-shape-ambiguous-armed`), `nonholder-stop-extra-record`
  (append an individually valid, chain-continuous record after the pure unknown-source stop of
  `r88-nonholder-stop`), and `mixed-stop-ambiguity-removal` (**control**: remove the later
  ambiguity in `r88-mixed-stop`).
- **Classifier is r88-wording aware.** The r88 disclosure line is
  `'<label>: provenance continuity and tick-order comparisons remain unassessed (input-structural finding, not sourced-canon); first stop: <cause> at <origin>'`.
  `reported` counts only non-disclosure `VIOLATION:` lines matching a defect-specific pattern;
  disclosure lines are excluded from the report matcher so a disclosure can never be miscounted
  as an independent report. `silent_pass = not reported and not disclosed`.

## Findings & named debt

- **H3 verdict — no harm found on the delivered tree.** 12/12 mutation cases exit 1 with either an
  independent report or a per-record disclosure naming the defect record and the first cause;
  **0 silent passes**; chain/tick comparison never resumes (0 comparison lines across all 12).
  All 8 r87 H3 mutations reproduce their r87 reported/disclosed/silent flags exactly. The corpus
  delta is purely additive: **0 exit-code changes and 0 fixtures losing a finding across 149
  delivered fixture directories.**
- **F1 — the r88 checker fixes two r87 silent passes it was not asked to.** Non-holder stop causes
  (unknown source, duplicate item id, non-item provenance, source-table-unavailable) now disclose
  the whole unassessed tail. `r87-probe-entry-shape` (a real chain break at `provenance[2]`,
  silently suppressed under r87) is the clearest case; `r87-probe-record-and-chain` entities `[3]`
  and `[6]` likewise gain disclosures with no lost findings. This is the C1 claim (iii) after-state,
  observed.
- **F2 — the violation-count drop from r87 to r88 is cosmetic.** r87 double-emitted the origin
  record's disclosure line; r88 emits one line per record. Every r87 report and every r87
  disclosure is preserved (`lost=0`), so the lower counts are not evidence of lost coverage.
- **D1 — NAMED DEBT: disclosure wording changed; r87-keyed consumers mis-read r88 output.**
  r87: `... comparisons unassessed because ambiguous holder identity X first occurred at Y`.
  r88: `... comparisons remain unassessed (input-structural finding, not sourced-canon); first
  stop: <cause> at <origin>`. Replaying r87 H3's classifier verbatim against the r88 checker
  yields **8/8 wrong disclosure flags and 4/8 false "silent pass" verdicts** (measured). Any seat,
  receipt, or scorecard row that greps the r87 string — including r87 H3's own
  `H3-mutation-delivered.json` `defect_record_disclosed_as_unassessed` field — must be re-keyed
  before it is cited as r88 evidence. This is a tooling/receipt-staleness debt, not a checker defect.
- **D2 — disclosure-only cases (no stronger "reported" property).** As in r87, tick inversion and
  holder swap are **disclosed, not independently reported**. The two new armed-record cases are
  reported only via their *shape/ambiguity* findings, not via the tick inversion or the swapped
  holder as such. No seat may claim "every mutation is independently reported" for these.
- **D3 — no residual test debt for this seat.** H3 changed no tracked file; the scratch tree is
  `git archive`-derived and the 12 mutation fixtures live only under `/tmp/r88-H3/corpus/fixtures/`.
  The corpus sweep's 149 directories exclude those 12 scratch directories.
