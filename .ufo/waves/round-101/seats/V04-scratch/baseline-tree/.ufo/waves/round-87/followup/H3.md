## Question
V4 (`patches/V4-mutation.json`) built seven provenance mutations from the F1/F2
masked-break patch fixtures in a scratch worktree at `28706dc` and showed the
pre-C1 checker masked every later chain/tick finding. H3 asks the same question of
the **delivered** tree: after C1 (`03c6746`) landed, is every one of V4's seven
defects either **reported** or **explicitly disclosed-as-unassessed** by the
delivered checker? A defect that produces neither is a silent pass and is harm.

## Method
V4's mutations are recorded as lambdas over the item's `provenance` array. Five are
based on `r87-masked-break-to-ambiguous`; two on `r87-f2-masked-break-from-ambiguous`.
H3 replays them by applying each defect to a scratch copy of the **landed** fixture
and running the **delivered** checker. Harness: `/tmp/r87-H3/h3_mutation_harness.py`
(fixtures built at `corpus/fixtures/r87-H3-*`, 8 cases: V4's 7 plus one cast-adapted twin).

### Base-fixture fidelity check (before mutating)
- `sha256sum /tmp/r87-V4/corpus/fixtures/r87-masked-break-to-ambiguous/entities.json corpus/fixtures/r87-masked-break-to-ambiguous/entities.json`
  → both `c7bb5fa49a9c9336254957846b022ce27568ad92aaf4205228375ea6d66d889c`.
  **The landed F1 base is byte-identical to V4's F1 base**, so mutations 1–5 replay exactly.
- `diff -q /tmp/r87-V4/corpus/fixtures/r87-f2-masked-break-from-ambiguous/entities.json corpus/fixtures/r87-masked-break-from-ambiguous/entities.json`
  → `Files ... differ`. **D1 — F2 base divergence:** the landed `r87-masked-break-from-ambiguous`
  is a *different authored bible* from V4's patched `r87-f2-masked-break-from-ambiguous`
  (integration renamed and rebuilt it). Landed cast: `mira/harbor/tower/dup`, 3 provenance
  records, ambiguity origin at `provenance[0]`. V4's base: `tala/nacre/willow/rill/sunwheel/aster`,
  4 records, ambiguity origin at `provenance[1]`. V4's two F2-derived mutations are therefore
  replayed by **defect semantics** on the landed fixture, and the literal appended record
  (`tala`→`rill`, source `f2-moon-ledger`) is now dangling/unknown by construction in the
  landed cast — so a **cast-adapted twin** (`tower`→`harbor`, `archive-ledger`, tick 5) was
  added, giving an appended record that is individually valid and chain-continuous. That twin
  isolates the disclosure question from the dangling-reference question.

**Chain comparison never resumes.** Grepping all 8 outputs for the two resume markers
`provenance chain break` and `must be greater than previous tick` yields `NONE`.
The tick inversion (M1), holder swap (M2), null mid-chain holder (M8), duplicate record
(M4), and cast-adapted appended record (M7b) are therefore *disclosed, not resolved* —
the checker states the assessment gap rather than silently skipping it or wrongly
resuming. V4's `no_chain_comparison_resumed` property holds on the delivered tree.

### Boundary worth naming
Two of the seven defects (tick inversion, holder swap) are **not independently reported**
by the delivered checker even after C1 — they are only disclosed-as-unassessed. That is
the designed C1 behaviour, and it satisfies the stated H3 criterion
("reported **or** explicitly disclosed-as-unassessed"). It would **not** satisfy a stricter
criterion of "every mutation must be independently reported". No seat should claim the
stronger property for these two cases.

## Findings
- **H3 verdict — no harm found on the delivered tree.** 8/8 mutation cases exit 1 with
  either an independent report or a per-record disclosure naming the defect record and
  the ambiguity cause; 0 silent passes; chain/tick comparison never resumes.
- **D1 — F2 base divergence (integration artifact).** V4's F2-derived cases were defined
  against a fixture (`r87-f2-masked-break-from-ambiguous`, 4 records, `aster` at `[1]`)
  that is *not* the fixture that landed (`r87-masked-break-from-ambiguous`, 3 records,
  `dup` at `[0]`). The V4 receipt's F2 indices and the `'aster'` cause strings must not be
  read as describing the delivered tree. H3 replays the semantics and adds a cast-adapted
  twin; both pass. No seat should cite V4's F2 output as delivered-tree evidence.
- **D2 — disclosure-only cases.** Tick inversion and holder swap are disclosed, not
  reported, by the delivered checker (see Boundary above). Any axis claim must use the
  "reported or disclosed-as-unassessed" wording, not "reported".
- **D3 — no residual test debt for this seat.** H3 changed no tracked file in the lane
  repo; the scratch tree is `git archive`-derived and the 8 mutation fixtures live only
  under `/tmp/r87-H3/corpus/fixtures/r87-H3-*`. The corpus sweep's 146 directories include
  those 8 scratch fixtures (138 delivered).
