## Findings & named debt

- The patch changes only tick-order wording. The B1/B3 unrelated-holder tick probes now report definite tick inversions; their identity-dependent chain-break findings remain qualified. The null-holder tick probe also becomes definite, while its adjacent null-from chain break remains qualified. Both delivered FN-2 fixtures are byte-for-byte unchanged by the candidate.
- Across the 160 canonical fixtures, the delta is empty: 160/160 tuples unchanged, zero exit changes, zero stderr changes, 32 clean and 128 violation exits, 399 violation lines. B1 and B2 each report the same canonical tuple-file hash, `80bdc3b45ea785ea7e04dea776d95da0bd6dad088f60ae95100f21039cae42ad`.
- **Scope interpretation:** S1 proposes keeping the tick qualifier when `transition_holder_details` is nonempty and removing the ambiguous-source gate. That still hedges a numeric tick inversion because a compared holder identity is unresolved. The persisted C1 claim and E1 wording panel instead say holder uncertainty does not qualify tick ordering; E1's template retains only the independent `chain_has_ambiguous_source` prefix. This patch follows the C1/E1 wording: no holder identity note or prefix on tick findings, regardless of holder resolution, while chain-break findings between unresolved non-null IDs remain qualified.
- **Named debt / scope:** X3 does not fix the null-from chain-break hedge; that is the other C1 alternative. It does not implement C1's FN-1/cause fixtures or manifest changes. The full C1 claim still requires integration of the selected alternatives and re-verification on the delivered tree.
- A full fixture archive was blocked by `/tmp` inode exhaustion. The sparse checker archive was sufficient for actual patch application and CLI replay; it was cleaned on exit. The tracked fixture tree matched HEAD, the B1 baseline receipt replay matched 160/160, and B1/B2 independently agreed.
