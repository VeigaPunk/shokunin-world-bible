## Findings & named debt

- **X1:** 159/160 corpus tuples unchanged; the sole delta is stdout wording on `r87-probe-source-prefix`, with exit 1 retained and five violation lines retained. X1 removes the ambiguous-source prefix from a tick finding when the compared holders are resolved, but keeps the identity qualifier when a compared non-null holder is unresolved. The targeted unresolved-holder tick probe demonstrates that remaining hedge.
- **X2:** null-only alternative; no corpus tuple changes. It correctly stops treating `None` as a zero-row unresolved identity, so the null-from chain finding becomes definite. It leaves the non-null unresolved-holder tick qualifier in place.
- **X3:** tick-only alternative; no corpus tuple changes. It removes holder-identity detail from tick ordering while preserving the independent ambiguous-source gate. The tick inversion becomes definite, but X3 alone still attaches the vacuous `None` identity note to the null-from chain finding.
- **Separability:** X2 and X3 apply in sequence and their measured composition changes none of the 160 corpus tuples; targeted probes show both independent fixes land together, while a genuinely unresolved non-null chain break remains qualified. The combined candidate's empty corpus delta differs from X1's `r87-probe-source-prefix` stdout delta. The independent tuple-change-set union is likewise empty (`union_equals_X1=False` in the observed output). This set comparison is not proof of code equivalence; the probes show X1 retains unresolved-holder tick hedging whereas the X2+X3 composition does not.
- **Clean-to-dirty risk:** no clean-to-dirty transition was observed for any individual patch or the X2+X3 composition; all preserve 32 clean exits, 128 failing exits, 399 violation lines, and empty stderr. The measured synthetic findings also remain exit 1. The edits change diagnostic qualification, not the existing violation conditions; this is strong evidence for the tested tree, not a proof for arbitrary future inputs.
- **Named decision debt:** X1 and X3 make different choices for tick qualification: X1 retains identity qualification for an unresolved compared holder; X3 removes holder-identity qualification and retains only source ambiguity. C6 measures the difference but does not pick which C1 wording should be integrated. X2+X3 is a composable alternative if both null-sentinel and tick wording changes are selected.
- **Artifact gap:** X2/X3 have no standalone `*-tuple-delta.json` files at the final timestamp; their reports and the independent C6 replay record the results. The 20-minute polling deadline had passed before X1/X3’s final patch revisions, which is why C6 reran against the final hashes above.
