## Findings & named debt

- Restore the baseline null-chain line and tick-order line when `from_holder is None`; `None` is not a resolvable holder ID, and the zero-raw/zero-indexed detail is vacuous. A valid tick inversion remains numerically decidable even when an adjacent non-null holder ID is unresolved.
- The unresolved non-null chain comparison remains qualified. Do not let that qualification leak to a tick finding, a later-null chain mismatch, or the first-record positional `from_holder null` requirement. The patch notes include all three definite forms.
- Preserve an independent ambiguous-source prefix if present; it is not an identity qualifier. The proposed definite wording is limited to the record's explicit structural comparisons, not story truth, source-text truth, or real-world chronology.
- Named debt: X1's current patch fixes nullable chain qualification and the first-record null requirement by gating chain qualification on non-null IDs, but it still qualifies tick inversions when a non-null compared holder is unresolved. X1's report acknowledges this interpretation difference; C1's text favors E1/X3's definite tick wording. Phase 2 should rule on the boundary and re-run the candidate on the delivered tree. No integrated-tree behavior claim is made. Fresh scratch A/B verification was blocked by `/tmp` inode exhaustion.
