# Editorial comparison — reported slate wording

Vela’s r72 account says she could read “KEEP THE EAST GATE CLEAR” on a slate (`vela-slate-note-excerpt.md`; source `vela-slate-note`). Jorin’s separately cited r73 account says he read “KEEP THE EAST GATE OPEN” on a slate (`jorin-slate-note-excerpt.md`; source `jorin-slate-note`). The reports give different wording. It remains unresolved whether they concern the same slate or inscription, when either reading occurred, or what relationship exists between the sources. Neither account establishes an inscription change or a direct contradiction.
