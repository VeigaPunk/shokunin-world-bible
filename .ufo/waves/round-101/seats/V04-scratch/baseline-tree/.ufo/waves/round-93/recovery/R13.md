Against r87's use of “possible” readings (`.ufo/waves/round-87/patches/M1-myth-cluster.patch:254-267`) and r88's explicit “editorial analogy” / “as a prompt” framing (`.ufo/waves/round-88/patches/M1-cluster2.patch:110-128,240-254`), a reader who extracts a cell from its table could mistake an interpretive reading for an assertion.
