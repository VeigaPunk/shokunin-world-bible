  The authored bible is a separate register of people, places, factions, and an item (`entities.json:1-85`), with a charter’s reciprocal alliance pledge (`relations.json:1-25`) and two incompatible accounts of the same Moon-Basin Vow location left unresolved (`timeline.json:23-43`; `contradictions.json:1-13`). Severity: high overclaim risk if Spence’s English account is generalized; the map and note expressly limit it.** The delivered excerpt attributes to Lewis Spence’s English prose the clauses “He called out ‘Earth,’” and “the solid land appeared” (printed p. 209; `corpus/fixtures/r88-m3-maya-cluster/source-excerpt.md:3-7`; [Project Gutenberg eBook 53080](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/53080)). The source excerpt is Spence’s report, not a K’iche-language text or an authenticated account of all Maya traditions.

  The associated fictional structure is a paired register: Aru and Evin have entries at the same tick but different places, which the README identifies as an occupied-slot near-miss, not a double-booking (`timeline.json:1-19`; `README.md:3-5`; `fictional-excerpts.md:10-13`). Severity: material source-limit risk if treated as translation-backed canon; current files keep it editorial.** The delivered Chamberlain translation excerpt locates the three clauses in Part III, “The Door of the Heavenly Rock-Dwelling”: “eternal night prevailed,” the deities assemble, and the lands “again became light” (`corpus/fixtures/r93-m2-kojiki-cluster/kojiki-chamberlain-excerpt.md:3-12`; [Terebess transcription](https://terebess.hu/english/kojiki.html)). The inspiration note says the comparison affects only editorial framing; all fictional claims remain supported by the newly authored M-17 archive record (`inspiration-note.md:5-11`; `archive-record-excerpt.md:5-12`).

  In that authored structure, Teva files the Lorn Index and Orni adds an “unmeasured” mark afterward (`relations.json:1-13`; `timeline.json:1-20`). The Kojiki translation is not an in-world source, no external names or events become fictional referents, and no R5 support or R9 manual-review PASS is claimed (`README.md:3-5,14-16`; `claim-excerpt-map.md:7-14,33-41`). The safe connection is a deliberately abstract editorial analogy, not an account of Japanese or Shinto tradition.
