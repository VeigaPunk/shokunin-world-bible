## Findings & named debt

1. **No silent pass anywhere.** 12/12 replayed + 4/4 new + 4/4 probes are reported-or-disclosed;
   `silent_pass` is `False` on all 20. The r89 checker is at least as strong as the r88 checker on
   the whole r88 mutation set.
2. **No finding lost.** All 12 replayed flags and exit codes are byte-identical to the r88
   delivered results; across all 160 delivered dirs the exit code never changed and no VIOLATION
   line was added or dropped on any changed dir.
3. **C1's combined summary works and is unbounded.** A valid-key record with two latching causes
   names both (`r89-c1-multi-cause-valid`), three-clause joins work (probe), and removing a cause
   removes its clause with no stale residue.
4. **FN-2 qualification is correctly conditional.** It appears for an unresolvable identity and
   disappears (control) once the identity resolves; the definite chain-break finding survives in
   both cases, so no finding is traded for the qualification.
5. **FN-1 wording covers both raw/resolved-index branches** for holders and for sources, in the
   local finding and inside a combined-summary cause.
6. **Named debt (not new, recorded as a bound).** `fn2-tick-qualification-superset` shows the
   qualifier is applied to **tick-order** findings too, i.e. a conservative superset: a tick
   finding that is arguably identity-independent is labelled `input-structural finding, not
   sourced-canon` with the identity note. This matches C1's own recorded bound; it softens the
   sourcing label of a still-emitted finding and does not suppress anything. Flagged here so the
   round's verdict can weigh it, not as a defect found in this seat's scope.
7. **Named debt (unchanged from r88 H3).** Defects that are *masked* by an earlier first stop
   (e.g. `holder-swap` and `tick-order-inversion` on `r87-masked-break-to-ambiguous`, where the
   record-0 ambiguity stops the chain) remain **disclosed, not reported**. That is the intended
   design; recorded so it is not mistaken for a pass.
8. **Probe fixtures are scratch-only.** The delivered 20 `r89-H3-*` fixture directories live in
   `/tmp/r89-H3/corpus/fixtures/`, and the re-verification set (the same 20 rebuilt plus the
   head-cause probe) live in `/tmp/verify-H3/corpus/fixtures/`; neither scratch was written to the
   lane repo (only the two JSON artifacts and this report were).
