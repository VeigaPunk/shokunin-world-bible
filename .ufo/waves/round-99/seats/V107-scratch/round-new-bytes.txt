This is a bounded knowledge record, not merged canon, not a finding, not an adjudication, and not a verdict on any character. It names one new observation class and the exact records that carry it. ## The observation class

**Clerk lapse record that closes no one** — a named office-holder's own first-person record that the crossing-window named in the original minute has closed, that the agreement was unfulfilled, that no claim was lodged, and that the matter stands as a historical open entry, where the record itself asserts no resolution, assigns no blame, and reconciles nothing. The class is defined by what the record says, not by what happened in the fiction: the note records a lapse and leaves the matter open. It does not establish that either account is true or false, does not find a breach, does not assign fault, and does not close the matter.

This is the fifth beat of the crossing-arc records: r95 carries Aru's reported non-performance, r96 carries Evin's second account, r97 carries Aru's acknowledgment that he received that response, r98 carries the harbor clerk's filing of both pending accounts with the matter recorded OPEN, and r99 carries the same clerk's record that the window named in the original minute has closed while the matter stays open. It is distinct from the r98 filing class (`corpus/knowledge/r98-clerk-scope-note.md`): r98's class rests on an institutional act of filing that asserts no finding; r99's class rests on a later record, by the same office-holder, that the crossing-window has closed and that no claim was lodged. The unresolved status is inherited and re-asserted. The close of the window is not a close of the matter, and it closes no one.

The denials in the note are negations, not positive assertions of a resolution, of blame, or of a reconciliation. "The agreement was unfulfilled" and "No claim was lodged" are the note's own statements about this crossing. They are not a breach finding and not proof about any record outside the note.
