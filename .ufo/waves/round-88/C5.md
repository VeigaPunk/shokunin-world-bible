## Findings & named debt

- **Potential over-disclosure / same-record precedence debt:** the block requires a rejected dict to arm on an ambiguous holder ID, but the existing shape gate itself already stops comparisons before holder analysis. In the probe, malformed shape and `to_holder="dup"` coincide at `[0]`. Reporting that holder ambiguity *as the first/operative cause* would over-attribute; the correct output must distinguish the first stop cause (shape-invalid record) from the additional fact that the recognized top-level holder reference resolves to duplicate entity IDs. The nested-garbage control also means detection must be limited to the defined top-level holder fields, not arbitrary nested keys. X1 is absent, so I cannot determine whether its actual detector or wording gets this distinction right.
- **Existing fixtures are expected to change:** the after-state explicitly adds disclosures for non-holder stops, so “first-cause tracking changes no existing outputs” cannot be assumed. The archived 145-row census is the before-state only. `r87-probe-record-and-chain` proves at least one existing tuple has a required disclosure delta; enumerate it against the actual X1 patch before accepting compatibility.
- **Wording cannot be certified:** no X1 wording was available to test against the earlier-invalid-then-later-ambiguous case or the same-record shape-plus-ambiguity case. A phrase such as “unassessed because holder identity is ambiguous” is overclaiming when malformed shape or another invalid record latched first. The message should name the tracked first stop and present later/simultaneous holder ambiguity as a separate structural observation; it must not imply custody, a chain break, or sourced canon.
