**F3 — r88 maya cluster: one clause is not mechanically resolvable (minor, real).**
`symbolism-clause-map.md` states "The map indexes the two exact clauses in the
delivered excerpt", and the r88 knowledge table's own rule says a fixed-string
search with "zero match fails". `grep -nF -- 'He called out ‘Earth,’'` on
`source-excerpt.md` returns **no match (exit 1)**: the map renders the nested
quote with single curly quotes while the delivered excerpt uses double curly
quotes (`He called out “Earth,”`). The clause is semantically the same sentence
and the second clause resolves, so this is a quotation-style transcription
defect, not an unsupported attribution — but the word "exact" is what fails.
The same paragraph's claim that the external source clauses "also resolve in a
delivered excerpt" is therefore 1-of-2, not 2-of-2.

none), so it is family-consistency debt rather than a novel r88 defect. Measured
exit on the delivered tree is 0.
