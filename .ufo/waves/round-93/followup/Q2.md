**The three r94 improvements** (mechanisms, not re-proposals; each with a
gate):

1. **Close the loop at the closure gate: make the wave contract a landing
   requirement, not a spec promise.** Land `wave-spec.json` WITH full
   roster rows (r91's `seats: 0` manifest shows even the precedent was
   partial) plus `corpus/tools/wave_contract_check.py` (declared ==
   enumerated IDs, no role-only/unnamed slots, artifact→seat mapping,
   scratch-budget numbers, helper hash) — and require W1's landing record to
   quote the validator's exit-0 receipt, with closure rejecting a round whose
   landing record lacks it. Rationale from evidence: three rounds of
   re-proposing (r90-Q2 P2 → r92-Q2 → r93 spec promise → failed) show
   dispatch-time promises do not survive; the only gate every round must
   pass is the closure commit. Gate: `wave_contract_check.py` exit 0 at
   dispatch AND its receipt verbatim in the landing record; target: zero
   rounds with declared ≠ enumerated, manifest absent at commit, or
   unnumbered scratch.
2. **Mechanical closure receipts: commands, not numbers.** W2's closure
   skeletons (and the commit-message template) must carry command
   placeholders; every number that reaches the LOG/SCORECARD/commit message
   is captured verbatim from a command run on the frozen tree at closure
   time (support-check counts, census deltas, verdict words). This removes
   the r88/r89/r93 claim-text class (improvement of record: A3) instead of
   correcting it after the fact. Gate: every numeric claim in the closure
   commit appears verbatim in a recorded command output from the round
   evidence; P2's 12-not-10 correction becomes the last hand-repair.
3. **One canonical debt registry, in-tree, closure-gated.** Single committed
   carrier (e.g. `.ufo/debt-ledger.json`) where every Qxx/Rxx/DEP id is
   defined once with origin round, status, and a re-runnable evidence
   pointer; a status flip to closed REQUIRES the pointer's command to
   re-run green on the closed tree, and the wave-contract validator
   (improvement 1) rejects closure text referencing an unregistered id.
   Evidence of need: W1 had to reconstruct dispositions from two sibling
   ledgers (`Q1-r92-debt.json`, `E1-debt.json`) plus four prose surfaces;
   the AST debt is stated in three places (work commit, Q1 proposal, P2
   section); K1-3 found a closure claim with no tracked carrier at all.
   Gate: at every closure, `git diff` of the registry shows exactly the
   status changes the round claims, each with a green pointer; target: zero
   re-derivation of known debts by the next round's methodologist.
