## Rationale and over-claim risk

- **R8 rationale:** creates a dated, neutral review record and asks for the creator's account even when the earlier note/map is absent. It distinguishes unresolved suspicion from confirmed general-motif use; only the latter can receive the element-level marker and bounded retention disposition.
  **Over-claim risk:** a plausible resemblance or reviewer concern is not proof of consultation, copying, rights infringement, or source-text support, and must not receive an element-level `external-only / unverified inspiration` marker as if use were established. Missing records do not prove non-use; do not withhold every generic motif or treat either delivery marker as a rights/canon ruling.
- **R9 rationale:** separates an unknown exact printing from an unverified source-work link. A reliable bibliographic tie can support a carefully bounded claim about the identified translation; the JDEP2 translator-only case lacks that tie and remains ineligible for R5 source support. This is distinct from paraphrase/summary and from an absent source text.
  **Over-claim risk:** the translator name and lexical anchor establish neither source identity nor translation fidelity. `CLEAN` is structural only. Do not infer a particular edition, original wording, rights status, or training permission.
- **R10 rationale:** defines the identity/field/occasion test and gives a separate durable home for a screened-out near-match, keeping the contradiction register restricted to supported incompatible pairs.
  **Over-claim risk:** a name/ID collision can be a false match, while different dates do not automatically prove distinct referents or compatibility. Require positive evidence for identity and same relevant occasion before registering; do not claim a live referent or populate the register from this synthetic example.

These are proposed governance words, not an implementation or a verification of the live cross-cluster registry. The actual tree's registry state and the round-90 J3 check remain separate evidence.
