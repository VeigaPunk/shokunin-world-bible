# QUALITY — lore admission bar

This file is the admission bar for lore corpus artifacts: knowledge entries under
`corpus/knowledge/` and sources rows under `corpus/SOURCES.md` (tier definitions
live in `corpus/SOURCES.md`; they are not restated here). The research method for
finding and mining sources belongs to the chousa lane and is out of scope for this
bar. Criteria are append-only: a superseded criterion is replaced by a new row that
names the row it replaces; rows are never rewritten or renumbered.

## Admission procedure

1. A candidate knowledge entry or sources row arrives with its evidence pointers.
2. Check it against Q01–Q10; any failed criterion is a reject reason, not a warning.
3. Record every admit and reject in `corpus/lessons/round-<N>.md` `## Gate`, naming the criterion and evidence line used.
4. Before the round closes, a non-author spot-checks ≥1 admit and ≥1 reject from that Gate section.
