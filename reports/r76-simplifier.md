# Round 76 simplifier — proposed registry-entry limit

## Verdict: NO-GO on a registry-entry candidate

I do not recommend inventing a registry entry for this round. The least-inventive candidate I considered—“Copy request entered for Jorin: gate notice.”—would add only another phrasing of the same task Jorin already reports (“I was sent to copy a gate notice”). It does not identify the requested notice or its wording, and so supplies no new answerable editorial question beyond asking for the same unspecified task details. A more informative entry would require arbitrary new particulars not supplied by the prior evidence. Naming it a registry entry could also make invented material look like documentary corroboration, despite a disclaimer.

## Evidence and comparison with precedent

R73’s `jorin-slate-note-excerpt.md` says: “I was sent to copy a gate notice. At the east steps, I read “KEEP THE EAST GATE OPEN” on a slate. I did not see who put it there.” Its `entities.json` claim `/7/claims/1` cites fictional, unverified `jorin-slate-note` at `sources.json` `/7`. The account gives no sender, request text, notice identifier, time, receipt, or completion. The r73 comparison already juxtaposes Jorin’s reported wording with Vela’s while leaving object, time, and source relationship unresolved.

R74’s decision table and evidence note already provide a useful bounded editorial agenda: evidence that might distinguish physical surfaces, ordered observations of one identified surface, or accuracy of a report tied to a particular occasion. Those are conditional evidence needs, not assertions that records exist. A second fictional record repeating “copy request / gate notice” would not satisfy any of them: separate fictional attribution is not authenticated independent corroboration, and a different source ID or document format would not establish provenance or independence.

R72’s precedent also treats a source row and cited claim as attribution, not authentication. The withdrawn r75 registry-entry idea is incident telemetry, not an executed proposal or supporting precedent; this r76 assessment is based on the fresh declaration and delivered r72–r74 material.

## Proposed disposition and limits

**Depth: NO-GO / unchanged.** No entry, clause map, or comparison should be added because the minimally invented version produces no distinct editorial question, while a more specific version would invent unsupported detail. **Coverage: unchanged. Calibration: unchanged on this report-only assessment**; no candidate was built, so there is no measured candidate effect. Revisit only if a genuinely distinct editorial question can be stated without borrowing documentary authority from the label “registry,” and a non-author reviewer finds it materially useful beyond R73’s account and R74’s existing evidence questions.
