# Round 58 simplifier — report-level contrast or no-go

## Independent judgment: DEPTH NO-GO

A Jorin account of seeing a strip he *took to be* Mira’s outbound route strip on the registry hook rack while he watched what he *took to be* Sella’s search is a sharper comparison than r53’s proposed satchel handling or r55’s proposed earlier placement. It places a positive reported visual sighting and a reported non-finding in the same described rack/search scene. But the useful contrast depends on Jorin identifying both the object and the activity: neither identification establishes that he saw the physical strip Sella sought, or that he observed Sella’s actual search. With those links expressly left unresolved, this remains two reports that may concern different objects and activities, rather than a material, independently interpretable difference. I would not admit depth on the hypothetical as framed.

The smallest defensible report-level comparison, if the proposal is retained for discussion, is only: **Sella reports not finding the named strip on the rack; Jorin hypothetically reports seeing a strip he took to be that named strip there while watching what he took to be Sella’s search.** This is a conditional observer difference, not a contradiction between aligned propositions. It may prompt a reader to distinguish “a witness reports not finding it” from “another witness reports seeing something he identified as it”; it does not establish that the reports address the same physical object or search. That generic epistemic distinction is already inherent in r23’s expressly bounded witnessed-absence class, so adding another uncertain observer does not yet demonstrate incremental depth.

## Assumptions, falsifier, and calibration risk

Assumptions: the Jorin text is hypothetical, newly authored fiction, not an existing excerpt or observed checker result. The proposed “while he watched what he took to be Sella’s search” does not independently establish Sella’s identity/action, a shared event, or a shared physical strip. A fixture tick would be an editorial index, not a synchronized clock. No source-ID or schema check can establish prose entailment or truth.

Falsifier for reconsidering GO: a non-author reader must identify a concrete interpretive gain that survives the possibility that Jorin saw a different strip and misidentified the activity, without converting either report into objective presence/absence. If the gain is only that another character supplies a positive sighting or that the scene sounds contemporaneous, the depth claim fails. A future artifact could be reassessed if it supplies a useful distinction that does not require those unverified links; this report does not prescribe or claim such an artifact.

Likely calibration harm if overstated: mapping Jorin’s sighting as the same strip or the same search, asserting that Sella missed an objectively present item, treating either witness as lying, or labeling the pair a contradiction would turn uncertain identification into fact. No objective strip presence or absence, actual search verified, same physical object, Sella lie, contradiction, removal, theft, custody, canon, rights/permission, or Blue Seal/carbon identity follows. Coverage and calibration are unchanged on the hypothetical; if those stronger claims were encoded or reported, calibration would be harmed. No candidate checker result is claimed.
