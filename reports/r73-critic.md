## Scope and evidence

I read the Round 73 declaration in `.shokunin/lore/LOG.md` (“Round 73 — depth claim; declaration before dispatch”), the active r72 fixture’s source/entity rows and Vela slate excerpt/map, inherited Jorin and Vela excerpts/maps in `corpus/fixtures/r43-jorin-vela-sighting/`, `check_bible.py`, and relevant Round 71–72 critic, reviewer, and adjudicator reports. This is therefore a declaration-level falsification, not a review of exact proposed Jorin wording, source/claim mapping, checker output, or the comparison note.

## Depth claim: discrepancy is possible, not established

R72 gives Vela’s attributed report that she read “KEEP THE EAST GATE CLEAR” on a slate; her claim separately marks the cramped-letter/hurried-writer thought as tentative and says she could not tell the writer (`r72-vela-slate-claim/vela-slate-note-excerpt.md`, `vela-slate-clause-map.md`, `entities.json` `/6/claims/1`). Its source row explicitly labels the report fictional and unverified (`sources.json` `/6`). Inherited Jorin says he stood at the registry doorway and saw Vela on the east steps during what he called Bell-7, but did not see who struck a bell (`r43-jorin-vela-sighting/jorin-note-excerpt.md`, `jorin-clause-map.md`). He does not mention reading a slate. Vela’s inherited account concerns hearing the bell and not seeing Sella or an envelope (`vela-note-excerpt.md`, `vela-clause-map.md`), not a slate.

Accordingly, a newly authored Jorin report with different words could create a **difference between two attributed reports**. That difference alone does not establish that both refer to one slate, one inscription, or one occasion; no inspected source links Jorin’s inherited Bell-7 sighting to Vela’s slate-reading report. The declaration correctly disallows a shared-time assertion and asks for an open question about identity/time/source. But absent candidate text, it is unproved that Jorin has a grounded reason to report a competing wording, that the comparison is narratively meaningful rather than an arbitrary second slogan, or that the editorial note avoids converting two claims into a contradiction. The common slate identity/occasion is precisely what must remain unresolved—not silently supplied by the comparison.

This is a potentially useful change from r72’s single report because it can expose uncertainty between accounts, but a different line from a second fictional source is not automatically depth. The depth case should rest on a legible, explicitly attributed wording discrepancy *and* the bounded uncertainty about whether the reports concern the same object/occasion, not on a presumed persistent inscription or an implied witness-to-witness dispute. If Jorin’s new report says only what he claims to have read without establishing any comparison within his account, the comparison is an editorial juxtaposition of unlinked reports; its value may be too slight to establish meaningful depth. If it calls the lines contradictory or assumes the same slate/occasion, that overstates the evidence.

## Structure and calibration limits

The declaration’s five-table clone with one new source and one Jorin entity claim, and no timeline event, relation, or contradiction row, avoids adding a shared tick or asserting a structured contradiction. The inherited Jorin account’s Bell-7 label is explicitly his own (`jorin-clause-map.md`); it does not authenticate a common occasion. Avoid importing that label into the new slate report or comparison as shared timing. Similarly, the east steps/registry doorway in the old report do not establish where or when he allegedly read the slate. A new excerpt may author a fictional report, but neither that authorship nor a source pointer verifies its truth.

`check_bible.py` requires entity claims to have nonempty statements and resolvable source IDs; it does not read the excerpts or maps, test quotation entailment, identify common referents, or decide whether accounts contradict. A clean run would be structural evidence only. The required exact clause map and non-author review must inspect the literal new source, claim, and comparison note. No contradiction-ledger addition is justified from unlike attributed wording without a supported common referent and occasion. This report makes no inference about canon, rights, or game mechanics.

## Provisional falsifier

**Depth: unproved pending candidate.** I would reject a depth claim if the second phrase is merely an arbitrary/redundant slogan, if the comparison note implies one persistent inscription or common occasion, if it calls the reports a contradiction without evidence of shared referent, or if the claim/map upgrades either account into fact. A narrow depth GO would require exact, source-cited Jorin wording and map to make a genuine reported-wording discrepancy legible while explicitly leaving identity, time, and source relationship open. Calibration can be judged only against those exact artifacts; no new timeline row is appropriate on the declared evidence.
