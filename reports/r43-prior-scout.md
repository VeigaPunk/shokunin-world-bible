## Material novelty assessment

The proposed Jorin account has a potentially new information unit: another named observer reports seeing Vela at a *specific* location (east steps). That is not R10’s institutional recording, R33’s envelope-placement observation, or R40’s own Vela-authored sensory account. However, it is only modest incremental narrative depth over R40: both accounts concern Vela’s reported east-steps location / Bell-7 setting. An additional attributed perspective can be narratively useful, but neither source is genuinely independent evidence merely because the character differs; both are authored fictional texts. Most importantly, no verified shared occasion has been established. Jorin’s account corroborates Vela’s reported location **only conditionally** if the two accounts refer to the same occasion and the text’s identity/place alignment is intended. Do not say Jorin corroborates the sound, bell identity, same strokes, Bell-7 chronology, or Sella/envelope absence.

## Risks and limits

- **False independence:** A different in-world voice is not an independently authenticated source; source/excerpt/IDs are project-authored.
- **Occasion conflation:** “what Jorin called Bell-7” is Jorin’s label, not a verified match to Vela’s Bell-7 or to the same occurrence. No common objective bell event/date/time is proved.
- **Location inflation:** Jorin reports seeing Vela at east steps; do not infer Vela heard the bell from there, was there for the whole event, or that the registry desk is the east steps.
- **Absence inflation:** Jorin’s stated non-observation of a striker does not establish no one struck the bell. Neither this report nor Vela’s non-sighting proves Sella or the envelope absent.
- **Canon/rights:** Citation linkage or a clean structural checker cannot establish truth, canon, ownership, or rights/AI-training permission.
