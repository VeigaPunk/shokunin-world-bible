## Falsification and assumptions

The strongest case for GO is that Jorin's proposed vantage makes the reports directly comparable in subject matter: a positive visual report during a search versus the searcher's non-finding. If a reader can use this juxtaposition to understand that non-finding is not equivalent to non-presence, it may clarify the epistemic limit of Sella's report. However, that limit is already explicitly recorded in r23's clause map (`.../witnessed-absence-clause-map.md:15-17`) and excerpt note (`.../sella-absence-note-excerpt.md:7`). The hypothetical adds another unverified report; it does not establish a new distinction beyond the existing non-finding ≠ world-absence boundary. The phrasing “while he watched what he took to be Sella's search” may imply simultaneity, but cannot prove he observed the entirety or relevant portion of the search, nor that Jorin and Sella meant the same individual search. “A strip he took to be Mira's” and “Mira's outbound route strip” are not a physical identifier; either report may concern a different strip or mistaken identification.

My NO-GO assumes material depth requires a distinct reader-relevant distinction or consequence, not merely a second attributed voice, more precise juxtaposition, extra map/rows, or structural validation. No inference is made about actual Sella/Jorin identity, same search, same physical object, objective rack state, truthfulness, or what either saw. No lie, contradiction, removal, theft, custody, canon, rights, Blue Seal/carbon identity, or relationship to the mark is claimed.
