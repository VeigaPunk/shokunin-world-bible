## Source-text, fictional attribution, and rights/canon boundary

A duplicated source ID means the checker cannot uniquely identify which in-world registry row a timeline reference names. It does **not** prove or disprove the referenced fictional location, event prose, source contents, witness reliability, or canon. Even a unique source ID is not source-text entailment: the checker reads registry metadata and row values, not the cited fictional text. `double-booked` can accurately describe a collision among supplied actor/tick/place fields while remaining unverified as a sourced narrative assertion. The qualifier must not call the collision false, erase it, or imply the referenced source supports it. Nothing here makes a claim about real-world source ownership, copyright/licensing, training permission, or rights; fictional fixture IDs and prose are not rights evidence.
