## Precedent and novelty assessment

R34 distinguishes “saw” as direct-observation **report** from “heard that” hearsay, and explicitly says the unchanged “reports seeing” JSON is unsupported by the hearsay excerpt rather than thereby false (`.shokunin/lore/reports/r34-scribe.md:7-15`). R41 is not just that modality switch: it asks whether a directly phrased, stronger source assertion is faithfully represented by a weaker attributed record. But the gain is narrow and must be described as **calibration of source-to-record wording**, not detection of truth or an objective contradiction.

R36’s controlled date near-miss holds all five JSON tables fixed while changing Sella’s excerpt from seventh to eighth day; it treats the stale JSON/ledger rationale as a text-record mismatch and declines to infer shared occasion or declare either side false (`.shokunin/lore/reports/r36-scribe.md:1-13`). That precedent is especially relevant: in both cases a changed excerpt no longer exactly matches fixed structured prose. R41’s proposed pair differs in epistemic strength, but not necessarily in logical compatibility: the unchanged tentative-belief statement could coexist with the stronger bell assertion. Claim novelty only as an **assertion-strength mismatch**, not a logical contradiction or disproof of the JSON.

R40 already bounds Vela’s tentative phrase, non-sighting, fictional occasion, and tick (`.shokunin/lore/reports/r40-scribe.md:7-14`; fixture map `vela-clause-map.md:6-16`). The r41 one-clause mutation is a materially testable near-miss against that positive, but unlike a checker rule it is a manually assessed prose distinction. Adjudication should ask whether this provides a useful new calibration case beyond r34/r36, not count the changed adjective/wording or preserved JSON alone as improvement.

## Risks

- **Overstatement:** “the excerpt proves the objective bell rang” confuses an authored fictional source claim with external fact.
- **Overcalling contradiction:** “I heard the registry bell strike twice” does not logically refute “I took the strokes to be the registry bell”; uncertainty of belief can coexist with a correct direct assertion. The defensible finding is mismatch in represented strength, not necessary incompatibility.
- **Understatement treated as falsity:** unchanged JSON wording may understate the excerpt’s assertion, but that does not by itself make the record false or establish Vela’s experience.
- **Citation laundering:** `vela-note` resolves structurally; it does not authenticate text, entailment, reliability, or truth. Checker success is not a prose adjudication.
- **Confusing claim subject/object:** stronger wording attributes bell identity to Vela’s fictional report; it must not be converted into narrator voice or objective timeline fact.
- **Collateral inference:** do not use bell wording, Bell-7/date, or tick 3 to corroborate Tarin/Sella, settle the ledger, or infer Sella/envelope presence/absence.
