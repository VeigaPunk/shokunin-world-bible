Keep the two source attributions distinct and trace each event to its own cited source. A resolving `source_id` proves referential integrity, not authorship, truth, direct witnessing, or independent corroboration (cf. `.shokunin/lore/reports/r33-schema-scout.md`, Risks).

## Risks

- A pointer can be syntactically valid yet semantically misleading: pointing a `place_id` endpoint at Tarin’s event says where Tarin is recorded, not where Sella is; pointing at an event with the wrong actor silently changes the subject. Check endpoint event actor and source alongside pointer resolution.
- The ledger’s `why` is editorial rationale. The checker’s acceptance of well-formed/resolvable pointers is not proof that the accounts concern the same bell, same moment, or incompatible positions. Exact text must support shared occasion and identity assumptions; otherwise retain the two reports as conflicting only conditionally.
- Preserve both source-attributed reports without selecting a canon winner. Do not infer envelope contents, Blue Seal/carbon/strip connection, motive, or independent truth. A first-person attribution and a valid source ID do not authenticate the account.

## Falsifiable check

On the proposed separate fixture, parse each contradiction endpoint and verify that its `event_id` exists, its `field` is exactly `place_id`, and the referenced event has the intended actor (`tarin` for Tarin’s harbor report; `sella` for Sella’s tower account); verify both values resolve to the expected existing places and each event’s `source_id` resolves to its own cited account. Then require the pair’s `why` and excerpt/map to explicitly tie both reports to the same named bell/occasion. The check fails if either pointer is dangling or targets another field, either actor/source is mismatched, or same-occasion wording is absent. Checker success alone must not be counted as proof of semantic contradiction or truth; retain both reports unresolved. Do not award a Blue Seal inference.
