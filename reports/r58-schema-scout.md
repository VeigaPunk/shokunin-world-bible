## Materiality, assumptions, falsifier

The potential gain is a concrete, same-scene *reported perceptual difference*: positive visual report versus a searcher’s non-finding. That can make the distinction between “did not find” and “was not there” more usable than an abstract warning. Assumptions still include that Jorin’s “what he took to be” Sella is the named Sella and that his strip identification corresponds to the strip Sella sought; the report does not objectively verify either, nor establish access, continuous rack state, or deception. A tick cannot cure those gaps. This is not a same-proposition contradiction: seeing a possibly identified strip while watching a possibly identified search is not logically incompatible with Sella reporting a non-finding.

Falsifier: if the actual hypothetical clauses do not explicitly carry both uncertainty-bearing identifications, or the structured mapping implies verified same strip/search/rack occupancy, reject.
