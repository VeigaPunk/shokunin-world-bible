## Precedent and materiality

R43’s admitted narrow comparison links Jorin’s reported sighting of the already named Vela at the east steps with Vela’s own reported location there, while expressly leaving shared occasion unproved (`.shokunin/lore/corpus/fixtures/r43-jorin-vela-sighting/jorin-note-excerpt.md:1`; `.shokunin/lore/corpus/fixtures/r43-jorin-vela-sighting/jorin-clause-map.md:5-10`). That is relevant precedent for attributed visual comparison, not a general rule that adding another observer improves depth. Here the explicit hedges “what he took to be” matter: the proposed account does not independently identify either the strip or whose search it observes. R35’s attributed-location disagreement likewise does not authorize turning this into an objective conflict. R53 and r55 show why a proposed connection that depends on unverified strip identity/sequence is not itself material depth; r55’s same-rack placement still did not establish presence at the search.

The candidate’s strongest possible value is a reader-facing reminder that a reported non-finding is not proof of objective absence. But the r23 map already makes that distinction explicit (`witnessed-absence-clause-map.md:14-19`). The hypothetical Jorin addition could illustrate a possible differing report, yet its asserted “while watching Sella’s search” relation is itself Jorin’s identification, and the object is only what he took it to be. No distinct consequence beyond that conditional juxtaposition is demonstrated. On the evidence available, depth is unchanged; coverage is not the target and unchanged; calibration is unchanged only if all hedges/attributions remain explicit. If a later representation upgrades the hedged identifications or report into verified object/search identity or objective rack state, calibration would be harmed.
