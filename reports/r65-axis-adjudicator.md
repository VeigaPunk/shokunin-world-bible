## Convention, materiality, and separate axes

The active relation pass calls `source(row['source_id'], location + '.source_id')` independently. The alliance pass then checks distinct, uniquely indexed faction endpoints and searches for an opposite `allied_with` row whose `source_status(candidate['source_id']) == 'unique'`; it does not test the current row's source status before emitting `lacks a sourced reciprocal relation` when the source table is loaded. R2 introduced the reciprocal input-row convention, not forward-source eligibility. R7 corrected qualification of the **reverse witness** and kept the local citation ambiguity plus missing qualified reverse finding; it does not establish a forward gate. R31 independently exercises ambiguous citation of a non-alliance relation; it adds no forward-alliance premise. R61 withholds source-index-dependent conclusions only when the entire source table is unavailable; loaded unknown/ambiguous IDs are different. No cited precedent decides that a present eligible forward row ceases to need reverse repair solely because its own ID is unresolved.

The proposed forward gate might avoid an apparent overclaim if the finding were understood as proof of a well-sourced *forward assertion*. But the actual message says only that the input row **lacks a sourced reciprocal**; it does not assert the forward citation is valid, treaty text supports it, or the alliance exists. The direct `unknown`/`ambiguous` citation finding identifies one repair; the row-local reciprocal finding identifies a *different* absent or unqualified reverse edge. Suppressing the latter may hide needed repair guidance, while no matched operator task shows it causes a wrong repair or that fewer lines materially improve decisions. The ambiguity side effects are source-index-wide, not evidence for gating one relation row. A future falsifier would demonstrate a concrete mistaken repair from the current two-pointer output and an improved repair from a narrowly gated candidate, without losing reverse-edge guidance and while preserving r7's qualified-witness behavior; no such candidate or result exists here.
