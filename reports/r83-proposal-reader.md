# r83 proposal reader report

The excerpt licenses only an attribution to Vela's testimony: she says she heard two strokes, took them to be the registry bell during Bell-7, and says, “I did not see Sella with an envelope.” The excerpt also gives her asserted location and describes Bell-7 as the sole seventh-watch registry-bell sounding on the seventh day of Embermonth, year 12; these are elements of the testimony, not independently established facts.

It does not establish that a bell objectively sounded, that Vela correctly identified the sound or its timing, or that anyone was objectively present or absent. In particular, “I did not see Sella with an envelope” reports not seeing Sella in that described circumstance; it does not say that she saw neither Sella nor an envelope separately, nor establish either one's actual presence or absence. The excerpt does not establish a sighting of Sella or an envelope.

**First editorial action:** In both fixed records, replace “reports not seeing Sella or an envelope” with “reports that she did not see Sella with an envelope.” This preserves the excerpt's narrower, paired formulation and avoids turning it into two independent non-sightings. The attribution to vela-note remains structural, not authentication.
