# Round 55 — independent precedent scout

## Verdict: NO-GO on material depth as framed

The hypothetical rack-placement account is more directly comparable to Sella’s reported non-finding than r53’s satchel-handling proposal: both would explicitly name the registry hook rack. That improves the *shape* of the comparison, but does not yet establish a material depth gain. Rack and item identity, the physical referent, and compatible occasion remain assumptions; a reported placement “as I departed” does not establish continuous presence until Sella’s later search, nor a state change or contradiction. Without a specific useful consequence surviving those assumptions, this risks turning a repeated two-account possibility into invented tension.
