# Round 80 critic — falsifying the depth hypothesis

## Scope

I read the Round 80 declaration in `.shokunin/lore/LOG.md`, the r77 latch candidate/reviewer/adjudicator, the r79 arrival-order candidate/reviewer/adjudicator, and the r73 slate comparison/reviewer/adjudicator. No Round 80 candidate is delivered here, so this is a critique of the declared test, not a verdict on unseen prose.

## Strongest NO-GO: two invented beats are not independent support

The declaration improves on r79 by requiring an action and a separately described response/consequence, two materially different scene presentations, and a consequence beyond the speaker’s stated preference. That is a useful materiality hurdle, but “separately described” must not be confused with independently warranted. If one fictional narrator invents both the action and the reaction, a citation ID and a clause map only attribute and locate both sentences. They do not make the response an independent reason to believe the action matters. An invented response that conveniently rewards, praises, confirms, or dramatizes the action can still be the r79 pattern in two beats: the author supplies a choice, supplies its validating rationale/consequence, then cites both as proof that the choice has depth.

The distinction is not that fictional writing must be externally authenticated to have artistic value. An author may intentionally invent an action and consequence, and a coherent scene may be worth writing. The narrower question is whether this fixture demonstrates a **material editorial decision or scene consequence**, rather than using its own authored premise as evidence for the premise’s importance. R79’s arrival-order sentence already contained a concrete choice and an explicit conditional consequence (“people who had waited longest would look as though they came last”); the reviewer found that consequence merely repeated the invented preference. Splitting a similar preference and its self-confirming effect into separate sentences would not pass. R77 likewise shows that a concrete action and a plausible generic character cue can remain slight texture.

A response can be meaningful without being true in any external or fictional-corpus sense. The candidate must show a legible internal consequence or tradeoff that constrains a distinct presentation choice—not claim that the citation proves what “really happened.” For example, the literal response might make two scene readings meaningfully diverge, and an editor could identify what each presentation causes the reader to understand or miss. But if all that survives is “the character is brave/careful/kind,” an abstract mood, or the narrator’s own explanation of why the action was good, material depth remains unshown. The declaration’s phrase “observable response” is not enough on its own: a response may be observable in the prose yet editorially inert.

R73 is a limited contrast, not a license to count any invented detail as depth. Its narrow gain was adjudicated from the comparison of two separately attributed reports with different exact wording and reading circumstances, while keeping their relationship unresolved. The gain was the bounded comparison/open question between accounts, not proof that the fictional reports were true or referred to one object. R80 must likewise identify what its response *changes* in scene presentation, beyond adding another authored fact.

## Concrete no-go test

Give a non-author reviewer the exact excerpt, bounded claim and the two proposed scene presentations, without editorial interpretation, title/role authority, unrelated slate linkage, or claims of established procedure. Require the reviewer to:

1. Point to exact clauses for the action **and** response, then state one specific, useful difference between the two presentations and the consequence for a reader/character/scene in each. The difference must be more than “include/omit the new detail,” “the character is [generic trait],” or paraphrase of the narrator’s stated rationale.
2. Explain what decision the response changes that would not follow from the action alone. As a counterfactual, remove or neutralize the response while holding the action fixed: if the same editorial choice and consequence remain, the response has not earned the claimed depth. If the only changed decision is whether to include the author’s own newly invented sentence, the warrant is circular.
3. Name a credible alternative presentation supported by the excerpt, not one manufactured by the reviewer’s backstory. If the response merely flatters/confirms the action, and no materially different reading or tradeoff is text-supported, reject.

**NO-GO** if the reviewer cannot independently identify that same non-generic consequence and decision from the literal clauses; if the “consequence” restates the action/preference or just rewards it; if it is removable without changing a meaningful scene choice; or if its value depends on speculative world rules, institutional authority, chronology, or r72/r73 linkage. This test does not require external corroboration. It tests whether the authored scene itself supplies more than self-justifying prose. A candidate passing it may support a narrow fictional/editorial depth judgment, not a finding of fictional-world truth.

## Keep evidence categories separate

- **Invented in-world attribution:** Source labels, cited claim, excerpt and exact clause map can show that an invented, unverified account is consistently attributed and that its quoted words align. They cannot authenticate the narrator, action, response, or consequence.
- **Citation-ID/structure checks:** A clean checker can show resolvable IDs and schema/structural compliance. It does not read or validate prose entailment, assess whether the response matters, or establish canon. Old-fixture byte/raw-CLI replay is regression evidence, not depth evidence.
- **Prose, truth, canon, rights, mechanics:** Human review can judge the prose’s internal scene consequence and editorial usefulness. Neither that judgment nor attribution establishes fictional-world truth or canon. Checker cleanliness and fictional labels establish no real-world rights, license, or permission; this round makes no game-mechanics finding.

## Provisional axes and reversal

**Depth: not demonstrated at declaration stage; provisional NO-GO pending a concrete candidate and the test above.** A two-beat structure is not itself a gain. Reverse only if a non-author reviewer can identify the same specific, useful, non-circular scene consequence from the literal excerpt and explain the changed choice under the removal test. **Coverage: unchanged** on the declared prose/source/entity move absent an actual new retrieval or validation capability. **Calibration: no improvement demonstrated** merely by fictional/unverified labels or careful attribution; assess the actual claim/map and prose for overstatement. Do not treat a speculative association as an asserted relation, but reject any actual unsupported claim of shared slate, occasion, institution, rule, or event.

This critique makes no finding about source-text truth, canon, rights, or mechanics, and does not judge an unseen Round 80 candidate.
