## Evidence and comparison

I did not find it there.” (`.shokunin/lore/corpus/fixtures/r23-note-class/sella-absence-note-excerpt.md:5`). The existing timeline's Sella note is tick 3 and depends on `departure`, not on an independently timed Mira placement (`.shokunin/lore/corpus/fixtures/r23-note-class/timeline.json:1-28`).

The r55 declaration proposes Mira saying she placed what she called “my outbound route strip” on the registry hook rack as she departed (`.shokunin/lore/LOG.md:936-941`). If newly authored and separately attributed, that would juxtapose a reported placement with a reported later non-finding at one named site. Unlike r53's proposed satchel note, this is not wholly disconnected from the searched location. But the repeated description is not a physical identifier: neither account verifies that both phrases designate one physical strip. Nor does “as I departed” plus Sella's “Later” supply a common clock, elapsed interval, or proof that placement preceded the search in a compatible way. Even if both descriptions are intended to refer to the same item, the placement report does not say it remained there until the search. The comparison therefore cannot support continuous rack presence, removal, theft, actual contradiction, or an explanation of the non-finding.

This is a modest improvement in *comparability* over r53, not demonstrated material depth. Once same-strip identity and compatible sequence are withheld, the durable reader-facing content is only that two fictional speakers make separately attributed statements naming a rack. The r55 question (“how does Mira's claimed placement relate to Sella's later non-finding?”) is intelligible as a question, but no consequence of answering it is identified beyond the unsupported identity/sequence possibilities. Adding a second source, claim, or event would encode that juxtaposition, not prove its material importance.

R43 is not a general precedent that conditional accounts qualify as depth: its admitted narrow comparison was Jorin's report of seeing the already-named Vela at a specified place where Vela reported standing, with shared occasion still unproved (`.shokunin/lore/reports/r43-critic.md`, “Evidence and assessment” and “Verdict”). Here the accounts do not report the same person's location or a witnessed encounter; the apparent relation is between purported placements of an object whose physical identity and intervening history are unknown. R30's textual-conflict precedent likewise cannot convert these statements into contradiction: unlike an explicit scoped negation of a selected relation, a reported placement and a reported non-finding can both be accurate without establishing any shared object or interval (`.shokunin/lore/reports/r30-critic.md`, “Falsification attempt”).
