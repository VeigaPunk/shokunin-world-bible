## Observed vs proposed

**Observed:** Current checks validate particular input references and tick comparisons. They do not inspect cited source text, establish that a dependency happened, infer real-world causal relations, adjudicate canon, or decide whether any work is licensed, in public domain, or permitted for training. A unique fictional `source_id` is pointer resolution, not corroboration, authenticity, authorship, ownership, or permission. The repository separates real-world methodological citations and rights/training questions in `.shokunin/lore/corpus/README.md:6`; no checker result answers those questions.

**Proposed (not implemented or validated here):** A graph-level cycle check could describe a cycle among uniquely resolved, schema-admitted event IDs as an input-structure property. Its diagnostic must be carefully scoped to those edges; it cannot say the represented events are causally linked in the story or real world. It must not promote malformed members, select among duplicate IDs, or turn negative-tick endpoints into certain edges. If every valid edge is required to point strictly to an earlier nonnegative tick, any directed cycle composed entirely of those edges is impossible; a cycle over otherwise valid uniquely identified edges necessarily includes an inversion/equal-order edge already reported. Thus adding a separate cycle line may offer no new decision and may merely repeat existing findings. A checklist entry being “unmeasured” does not establish material coverage gap.

## Material coverage verdict

**NO-GO on material coverage, on present evidence. Do not implement a cycle diagnostic in this round.** The proposed condition is structurally describable, but the record supplies no demonstrated editorial decision improved beyond existing edge-level inversion/invalidity findings. For strictly earlier nonnegative edges the cycle cannot occur; for cycles containing an order-violating edge the checker already identifies at least one local inversion. Graph identity could still be useful to an operator, but usefulness is not demonstrated by the checklist or by calling a clean checker “acyclic.” No runtime probes were performed by this scout; this is a source/fixture-history and scope judgment, not an empirical claim about checker output on an unrun fixture.

**Falsifier / reconsideration threshold:** Produce a minimal, schema-valid, uniquely-IDed cycle case and an acyclic control with the same number and kind of existing edge findings; show an actual editorial task where naming the cycle changes a decision or action that the edge-local inversion list does not, without extra collateral findings. Include controls for ambiguous IDs, negative ticks, and malformed dependency members to show the graph rule neither invents nor suppresses edges. Independently replay baseline behavior and review the operator value. Until then, coverage is not materially demonstrated. Even a future GO would only concern coverage of JSON input-graph shape; it would not establish fictional causality, source-text support, canon, real-world rights, or training permission.
