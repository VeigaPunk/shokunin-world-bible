Under the proposed excerpt wording “I saw someone I took to be Vela,” Jorin still reports seeing a person, but only tentatively identifies that person as Vela. The fixed JSON proposition “Jorin reports seeing Vela” is **logically compatible**: the person may indeed be Vela, and the report may be accurate. It is not, however, directly supported at the same identification strength by this wording. The excerpt does not directly identify the person as Vela; it reports Jorin’s tentative belief. It does not show the person was not Vela, nor objectively establish any alternative identity. Do not recast this as a contradiction, false JSON, or a conclusion that Jorin saw someone else.

## Distinguishing prior art

The distinction from r34 is not “saw” versus “heard that”: r34’s hearsay near-miss no longer supports a claim of direct sighting (`.shokunin/lore/reports/r34-modality-scout.md:1-4,15-20`). Here the visual observation remains direct as a report; its *referent identification* is tentative. Yet r41 already contrasts a tentative source identification with a stronger direct identification while unchanged JSON remains logically compatible but under-specific (`.shokunin/lore/corpus/fixtures/r41-vela-direct-bell/strength-contrast-map.md:1-3`; `.shokunin/lore/reports/r41-strength-scout.md:1-10`). So this is a directional and referential variant of an established confidence/strength mismatch, not clearly a novel calibration axis. The r41 case is sound prior art against claiming novelty merely because the identified thing is a different kind of entity.

R43’s cross-account location connection is explicitly conditional: shared “Bell-7” labels do not prove shared occasion or chronology (`.shokunin/lore/corpus/fixtures/r43-jorin-vela-sighting/jorin-clause-map.md:9-13`; `.shokunin/lore/LOG.md:718`). Under the tentative-person wording, it is weaker still: the report may or may not refer to Vela, so it cannot straightforwardly support the prior cross-account location link. This does not modify Vela’s own attributed location report or establish a non-Vela person, common occasion, or canon.
