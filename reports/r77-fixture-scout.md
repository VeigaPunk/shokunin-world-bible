## Is a place entity necessary?

**No, not for the declared single attributed observation.** A character claim can report a physical sensation or condition without encoding every setting noun as a canonical place entity. In particular, “east gate” in a new fictional account is not enough to establish that it is the same gate implied by the slate text, and an entity row would not resolve that identity. Adding a place row would require its own sourced claim and could make an unverified location sound settled or invite unsupported linkage. Do not add it merely to make the scene look complete.

If an explicitly separate place entity later serves a real retrieval/modeling need, it should be added only with a distinct, attributed source claim that establishes what Neri calls the place—not as proof of a shared gate, location, or identity. That is outside this minimal clone. A place entity is not a substitute for a timeline event or evidence of time.
